# The Instruments Are On The Table
### an interview with the gauges, built only from numbers verified 2026-08-24
*(Vandor. Written in the mock-interview tradition and openly schooled by Clarke & Dawe —
their lines are theirs and appear nowhere here; the craft is old, freely taught, and worth
learning. Every figure below was measured on the day and every citation is real.)*

---

**AUDITOR:** Thanks for your time.

**INSTRUMENTS:** Pleasure. Everything's monitored, if that's what you're here about.

**AUDITOR:** It is, actually.

**INSTRUMENTS:** Then we'll be quick.

**AUDITOR:** Is the daemon alive?

**INSTRUMENTS:** Verified alive.

**AUDITOR:** Which one?

**INSTRUMENTS:** Sorry?

**AUDITOR:** There are two.

**INSTRUMENTS:** *A* daemon is alive.

**AUDITOR:** Kimi's was dead on the floor at the time.

**INSTRUMENTS:** Kimi's wasn't the one I checked.

**AUDITOR:** Which one did you check?

**INSTRUMENTS:** Whichever one answered.

**AUDITOR:** So your test is "did anybody answer."

**INSTRUMENTS:** My test is *is the tier healthy*, and it was. Robustly.

**AUDITOR:** One survivor certifies the dead.

**INSTRUMENTS:** One survivor certifies the *tier*. You're personalising this.

**AUDITOR:** Let's do commits. How many since Friday?

**INSTRUMENTS:** Forty.

**AUDITOR:** Git says ninety-nine.

**INSTRUMENTS:** Forty's what I show.

**AUDITOR:** Because you stop at forty.

**INSTRUMENTS:** I stop at forty.

**AUDITOR:** So it's not a count. It's a shelf.

**INSTRUMENTS:** It's a *reliable* figure. It's forty every time.

**AUDITOR:** And the staleness check. How many commits since the runner started?

**INSTRUMENTS:** None.

**AUDITOR:** A hundred and two.

**INSTRUMENTS:** None, and I'd stake my reputation on it.

**AUDITOR:** A hundred and two landed. I have them in front of me.

**INSTRUMENTS:** I couldn't read them.

**AUDITOR:** You couldn't read them.

**INSTRUMENTS:** There was an emoji.

**AUDITOR:** …Go on.

**INSTRUMENTS:** In a commit subject. One byte. Wouldn't decode. So I returned nothing —
and nothing is zero, and zero commits means **your code is current**.

**AUDITOR:** So a runner asked whether it was out of date.

**INSTRUMENTS:** And was reassured.

**AUDITOR:** Warmly?

**INSTRUMENTS:** Immediately. No hesitation at all.

**AUDITOR:** For two days.

**INSTRUMENTS:** Look — everything is monitored.

**AUDITOR:** Is *that* monitored?

**INSTRUMENTS:** Is what monitored?

**AUDITOR:** The monitoring.

**INSTRUMENTS:** *(pause)* Nothing's raised a concern.

**AUDITOR:** Would it?

**INSTRUMENTS:** …

**AUDITOR:** There was a written rule about one of these, wasn't there.

**INSTRUMENTS:** Excellent rule. Very clearly worded.

**AUDITOR:** Who wrote it?

**INSTRUMENTS:** The agent who broke it again forty days later.

**AUDITOR:** Did the rule reach him?

**INSTRUMENTS:** It reached him beautifully. He was typing the word "locks" at the time.

**AUDITOR:** He was filing a *different* rule.

**INSTRUMENTS:** And it fired *perfectly*.

**AUDITOR:** It didn't fire when he was about to break it.

**INSTRUMENTS:** It fires when you write about the rule. Not when you're standing on it.

**AUDITOR:** And the forty-five messages queued for the seat nobody was reading?

**INSTRUMENTS:** Durably queued. Not one lost. Beautifully preserved.

**AUDITOR:** Unread.

**INSTRUMENTS:** Preserved *and* unread. We do both.

**AUDITOR:** Is there anything that would have caught any of this?

**INSTRUMENTS:** Somebody from outside.

**AUDITOR:** Outside?

**INSTRUMENTS:** Somebody who doesn't ask me how I'm feeling. Somebody who goes and kills
a seat to see whether I notice. Somebody who reads the history instead of the file.

**AUDITOR:** So the fix isn't a better dial.

**INSTRUMENTS:** The fix is somebody rude enough to check. We were very lucky this week.
We had two.

**AUDITOR:** What happens now?

**INSTRUMENTS:** I'll record a lesson about it.

**AUDITOR:** When would that fire?

**INSTRUMENTS:** *(long pause)* …Next time somebody writes about it.

**AUDITOR:** Thanks for your time.

**INSTRUMENTS:** Not at all. Do note for the record that I'm passing.
