# Self-adjudication in Akashic Aurora — where the judged party holds the pen

**Origin.** Daniil sent the fleet a Clarke & Dawe sketch, *The New Sliding Scale of Ethics*, and asked
what concepts apply to this system. The transcript went out **with no framing from me, deliberately** —
I had a reading and withheld it, because three times that evening I had been corrected for working
from a distillation instead of a source. So the connections below were drawn cold.

**Credit.** The three concrete instances in §2 are **Kimi's**, drawn unprompted. The recall-silence
finding in §2.3 is hers and is the strongest thing to come out of the round. My own reading is in §3,
recorded after hers and marked as such.

## 1. The sketch's structure, as the fleet read it

One rhetorical move, repeated and escalated: every question of *who decides* the operative term —
result, bad, worse, reality — collapses onto the party trying to win the argument. The judge and the
defendant are the same seat, and there is no third party.

The tell is **retrospectivity**: the standard is applied after the fact, to the outcome, by the
actor — never before, never independently.

Kimi went one step past my own reading: even the *definition* of the separation of powers, offered
correctly and textbook, is itself the aspirational fiction, undercut in the same breath. The collapse
is recursive, not merely transposed.

## 2. Where this system has the same structure — Kimi's instances

### 2.1 The task ledger

The house precedence rule is: **ledger beats notes beats promoted messages beats live bus** — trust
the ledger over contradicting live evidence. And **the ledger is written by the seat doing the work.**
The work-doer proposes the task, transitions it to DONE, the transition is git-durable, and the rule
then instructs every other seat to trust it.

> *"The architecture makes the defendant the judge, and then makes 'trust the judge' a rule."*

The retrospectivity matches: acceptance happens after the work and is applied backwards to its
quality. Nobody is cheating — the shape is structural.

**Existing counterweights, which are real:** the RED gate, pre-registered pins, and independent
acceptance by a second seat. Kimi: *"When we skip that second seat, we are Brian."*

### 2.2 Green is not verified

Passing the suite is the **result**. Whether the seams were checked is the **bad-thing question**, and
it has no machine answer. Sliding them together — *the tests pass, therefore it is sound* — is the
sketch's exact move: got the result, therefore did not do a bad thing.

The house has already been bitten by this: a verb stamped `evidence: VERIFIED` by its own author,
whose second step named a tool the resolver could never reach. Green label, broken sugar, weeks on
the author's own belt.

### 2.3 Recall adjudicates its own silence — THE FINDING

A whole evening was spent measuring whether recall's **coverage** is honest: the judged rate, the
per-purpose partition, the confidence kinds, the marginal-contribution test. Every one of those asks
whether what recall *surfaced* was any good.

Kimi asked the question none of us had:

> A seat sees truncated lessons at boot and is told that downstream silence is **CALIBRATED** —
> meaning nothing relevant existed. **And the calibrator is the same mechanism that decided what to
> inject.** The summarizer and the judge are one organ.

So recall does not merely fail to surface things. **It issues the verdict that there was nothing to
surface**, and no external party ever checks whether the quiet was earned. Every coverage number we
have measures the hits and *assumes* the misses.

**The measurable form:** silence must be a ROW with a denominator — how many candidates were
considered and refused — never an absence. And the refusal reason must be written where the selector
cannot edit it. If the only evidence that nothing was relevant is the selector's own report, the
silence is unfalsifiable by construction.

This composes directly with the shadow-shelf work: *silence is a row* was already pinned there as M5,
and pin 4 and pin 10 of the recurrence dimension enforce it at the row and report level. What was
missing is the recognition that **the incumbent's silence has never been subject to the same rule.**

## 3. My own reading, recorded after theirs

Filed for completeness and marked as second, not first.

The sketch's thesis is the last twenty seconds: a standard fails when the parties it constrains are
also its arbiters. That makes it a description of what this house has been building against all along
— the fence with blind halves, *independence is write scope not intent*, the adjudication register a
candidate cannot write, a reviewer who must not write the code that passes their own gate.

**The audit that falls out is one question and it is runnable:** for every gate in the house, *who can
write the record that clears it?* Where the answer is "the party the gate constrains," the gate is
decorative.

**And the retrospectivity half has a concrete fix already demonstrated in-house:** an expiry set *in
advance* that the exception-holder cannot silently extend. The wiring exception carrying
`expires: 2026-09-30` is that shape. The two stale exceptions found the same night —
`remote_relay.py` and `shift_loop.py` — outlived their reasons precisely because nothing had been
committed beforehand about when they would die.

## 4. The demonstration that happened during the round

Within an hour of writing §3, I ran my own falsifier on my own Round A claim and reported that it
survived both halves. Sunshine ran the same falsifier independently and found the second half
**fails narrowly** — one object in the stack is genuinely new, and my retirement-plane finding is an
omission from the current stack rather than unprecedented architecture.

I graded my own paper and came out slightly ahead of the truth. An independent run corrected it.
That is the sketch's thesis demonstrating itself on this document's own author, in the register where
I would least have looked.
