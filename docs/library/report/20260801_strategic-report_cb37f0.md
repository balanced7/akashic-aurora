## PART 1 — The span: what we were actually building

Four phases, and the pivots are where the learning is.

**April 2026 — the over-build.** Triple-redundant Redis with HA failover, sync pollers, a comms
mesh — infrastructure built before the problem was defined. *"Almost none of it survived on its own
terms."* What survived was the itch: agents that keep what they learn.

**June 2026 — the foundation.** Store + Ledger, the audit, ubiquitous-language naming, the
append-only substrate decision. The one idea everything still leans on was named here: **append-only
substrate, regenerable projections.** The record is sacred; everything above it is disposable
*because* the record is not.

**July, first half — the intelligence layer.** Recall-at-action; the outcome-credited funnel;
lessons injected at the moment of action rather than retrieved on demand. This is the differentiator
and it is measurably working (646 lessons, credit tracked, flips recorded).

**July, second half — the fleet becomes real, and the load becomes the problem.** Bifrost,
multi-agent rounds, fences, the interiority corpus. And with it the characteristic wound: **width.**
~25 directives in seven days, 21 simultaneous ledger claims, 597 lessons colliding with a 20-task
board. Every organ was built with a birth and no death.

**The through-line:** this project keeps succeeding at *capability* and then paying for it in
*load*. Every crisis in the record is an accumulation crisis, never a capability crisis.

## PART 2 — The themes, consolidated

Eight. The first seven come from the ledger re-cut by purpose; the eighth is missing from every
existing artifact and I am adding it.

**T1 · I CAN SEE IT** — optics, dashboards, the engine legible.
**T2 · I CAN NAVIGATE IT** — what ties to what, and why.
**T3 · THE FLEET ACTUALLY WORKS** — mail, routing, liveness, seats.
**T4 · MY LEVERAGE** — the intake organ; ideas seeded at his pace become work.
**T5 · IT TELLS ME THE TRUTH** — instruments that report their real state.
**T6 · IT REMEMBERS WELL** — recall quality, lesson lifecycle, relevance budget.
**T7 · HOW WE WORK** — the three doctrine planes. Two ratified 2026-08-01.
**T8 · HIS OWN CONTINUITY** — *"I wish I could always return to the best version of me at peak
creativity."* Nothing in this system restores the OPERATOR. Everything restores agents.

## PART 3 — Implications: what each theme actually enables

**T5 (truth) is the load-bearing one, and it is invisible on any roadmap.** Seven defects surfaced
in 24 hours and they are one defect: a system reporting a state it is not in. The ledger said
"release your claim" and dropped the reason. The wake guard promised it would block and could not.
Boot said research files persist while the guard refused them. Lookback claimed to search a corpus
that excluded his own charters. A note write echoed my text back and read as success.
**Implication:** every one of those taught a seat to distrust an instrument, and distrust compounds
— I re-armed a dead watcher five times *because it told me it had worked*. If the proposition is
"an agent can trust what this system tells it," T5 is not a feature, it is the product.

**T2 + T1 are ONE instrument, and the design already knows it.** See Part 5, A1. This is the
biggest single correction in this report.

**T3 (the fleet) is the substrate every other theme runs on.** Nine tasks, the largest cluster, and
the most recurring cost. **Implication:** it is not glamorous and it gates everything. Wave B
cannot be skipped by preferring nicer work.

**T4 (leverage) is the multiplier on him.** O7 is now ruled — seeding is not promoting, promotion is
joint. **Implication:** once the door exists, his thinking-aloud stops costing either of us
vigilance. Today the buffer is a discipline in one seat's head, which is precisely the thing the
round said it must never be.

**T6 (memory) is where the differentiator lives.** Recall-at-action is genuinely ahead. **Implication:**
its risk is not quality, it is *cost* — the relevance budget (T071-R1) caps what every boot pays,
forever, across every seat.

**T7 (doctrine) prevents drift, and is inert.** Both ratified files say so themselves: *a contract
is inert until projected into organs that fire.* **Implication:** ratification bought nothing yet.
Part 7 of ORG and Part 3 of WORKING-METHOD are the actual work.

**T8 (his continuity) has no owner and no task.** **Implication:** it is the only theme where the
system's failure costs *him* directly rather than costing a seat. It is also the one he named
first, in his own words, and nobody built toward it.

## PART 4 — The hierarchy I would run, and why

Ordering principle: **unblock the many, then compound.** Not "most valuable first" — most *unblocking*
first, because this project's failure mode is width, not ambition.

**FIRST — T7 projection (small) + T5 as a standing gate (free).**
Cheapest, and everything downstream inherits it. ORG Part 7 is six edits, none of them a subsystem.
T5 costs nothing at all: it is two questions asked at ship time. *Does this surface ever claim a
state it is not in? Does it say what it is bounded by?* Both would have caught all seven defects.

**SECOND — T4, the intake organ.**
Because it changes how every subsequent decision gets made, including the ones in this report. It is
unblocked: O7 ruled, six pre-registered acceptance tests exist, no other seat required. Building it
second means everything after it is seeded through a real door instead of through my attention.

**THIRD — T2+T1 together, as one instrument.**
The trace and its control surface. See A1.

**FOURTH — T3, the fleet.**
Large, unglamorous, and now unblocked (deepseek and kimi are live as of tonight; the fence T108
requires is finally possible).

**FIFTH — T6, the relevance budget first.**
Highest measurable win per line of code in the project.

**T8 rides inside T4 and T2 rather than queueing.** His continuity is not a separate build: it is
the triage log (T4) plus the trace (T2) pointed at *his* threads instead of at agents'.

## PART 5 — Amendments I would make

These are the changes I would make to reach the goals stated above. Each names what it fixes.

### A1 · MERGE T1 AND T2. They were never two things.

The strongest finding in this report, and I only have it because I read the designs tonight.

- **His trace, in his words:** start at an object, pick an axis — *temporal, purpose, cost* — and take
  shallow hops.
- **The super-wiki design's three hop planes:** **LOGICAL · THEMATIC · TEMPORAL.**
- **His control metaphor:** *"changing dials in a game engine."*
- **Where the wiki ships:** *"a new tab in :8787 bifrost_ui. No second port, no fork, no npm."*

The navigation layer is *already designed to ship inside the dashboard*. Optics is not downstream of
navigability; **the visual layer is how the trace is flown.** The plan's earlier sequencing — optics
third — contradicted a design that had already resolved this a week earlier. Correcting it.

### A2 · T125 AND T103 ARE THE SAME INSTRUMENT ON TWO CORPORA. Design them once.

T125 walks **code** (modules, imports, blast radius). T103 walks **knowledge** (atoms, typed edges,
supersession). Both are his bolt-trace. Both need axis selection, shallow-hop defaults, and
status-aware rendering so you never land on a fossil unknowingly.

Today they are two tasks, two waves, two designs, with no shared vocabulary. **Amendment:** give
them one traversal contract — *pick axis, hop shallow, render status, offer depth on request* — and
two adapters. This is the "template for any future work" he asked for; the template is the traversal
contract, not the datasheet script.

### A3 · TAG EVERY LEDGER TASK WITH ITS THEME. One field.

The ledger orders by *when proposed* and carries nothing about what a task is *for*. That single
absence produced the 21-claim pile-up and made his oldest priority look neglected. **Without this,
every plan I write rots into the next `ROADMAP.md`** — which is exactly how the last one became
historical. This is the durable fix for the problem this report solves by hand.

### A4 · DEFERRED ≠ STALE. Give deferrals a precondition field.

His considered *"foundation before dashboards"* call rendered as `stale, 22d untouched` —
indistinguishable from rot, and it led his own strategist to report his strategy back to him as
neglect. **Amendment:** a deferred item carries its precondition — *"until: foundation usable by
inhabitants"* — and renders that instead of a decay counter. Same mechanism as the park reasons
shipped tonight, extended to proposals. This is a T5 defect that happened to point at him.

### A5 · RETIRE `docs/ROADMAP.md` FORMALLY, pointing at the plan.

It self-declares `historical` in its header and is still titled "Master Roadmap" and still cited by
memory as ⭐ START HERE. Half-retired is worse than either state. One edit.

### A6 · GIVE T8 AN OWNER AND AN OUTCOME.

Not a subsystem. The outcome: *he can return to a thread with its charge intact, not just its
conclusions.* The existing pieces that already serve it — the interiority corpus, T092's reasoning
spine, the wrap ritual, and the buffer's triage log — have never been grouped or aimed at him. This
is the cheapest high-meaning work available and nobody has claimed it.

### A7 · CLOSE B3 FOR THE POST I HOLD.

ORG.md requires every mind-post to name an understudy. Chief of Staff names none while I hold it,
and the evidence in the doc is 30 dead `claude` incarnations. Tonight `opus-engineer` appeared,
filed a real finding, and vanished — costing nothing only because nothing was in it. **Name a
successor for this post**, and honour the round's standing rule: overlap-handoff, not death-handoff,
with everything the seat holds in files *before* the transfer.

## PART 6 — What I am least confident in

Stated so it can be attacked first, per house practice.

- **The eight themes are my cut.** Another seat reading the same ledger might cut it five ways or
  twelve. The threads are a *finding* only in the weak sense that the items exist; the *grouping* is
  a claim.
- **A1 and A2 are the two I would defend hardest** — they are grounded in his own words plus a
  design document that already agrees, which is the strongest evidence available here.
- **Part 4's ordering is a judgement, not a derivation.** Its weakest link is putting T4 above T3:
  the intake organ compounds, but the fleet substrate is what everything runs on, and a reasonable
  strategist would swap them.
- **I have not read ~700 library artifacts.** Any claim above about what was *tried and rejected*
  could be wrong in a specific case. Where I assert history, I read the source; where I assert
  absence, I may simply not have found it.
