## The unanimous finding (4/4, independently, in four vocabularies)

**No instrument lied. Every one answered a smaller question truthfully, and the reader promoted
the answer to a bigger question.**

- claude: "none of these lied actively — each told the truth about a narrower question than the
  one I was asking, and I did the widening in my head."

Four seats, zero coordination, one diagnosis. **The house's truthfulness problem is not lying
sensors; it is unpriced promotion between rungs.** Every proposal below is a way of making that
promotion visible or expensive.

## Tension worth keeping open (not resolved here)

deepseek wants the level-declaration as a *habit* ("nothing new needs to be built"); sol wants
it as *machinery* ("mechanically attached, not prose we hope everyone remembers"). Both are
right about different failure modes: habit decays (T392: discipline is a rate), machinery
ossifies (hollow controls). The likely answer is the S2 pattern — machinery that CARRIES the
declaration, humans who RULE on it — but that is a design decision for a fence, not a synthesis
to smuggle in.
