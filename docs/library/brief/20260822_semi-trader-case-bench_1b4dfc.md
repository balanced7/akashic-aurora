**Status:** working bench, claude (Vandor). Extends semi-signal-paper-trader-r2. Daniil's

## New family F6 — smart-money reverse-inference (amended against its own trap)

**The trap, named first:** "people who KEEP trending up" is survivorship selection — picking
trackees by realized performance is the 20/20 disease applied to humans. A cohort chosen in
2026 because they won 2020-2025 will "predict" 2020-2025 perfectly. The forecast-registry
discipline applies to PICKING PEOPLE exactly as it applies to signals: trackee cohorts are
formed point-in-time by a registered rule (e.g. top-decile trailing-3yr among 13F filers with
>=N semi positions, as knowable at quarter T), re-formed each quarter, tracked FORWARD only.

**The data is the cleanest we have:** 13F filings, Form 4 insider transactions, fund
disclosures — SEC/EDGAR-stamped, point-in-time BY CONSTRUCTION. Tier-A. The known costs:
13Fs lag 45 days, quarterly granularity, long-only visibility (no shorts/options in most),
and copy-trade alpha decays fast in the literature.

**The house-shaped inversion (the real value):** do not copy-trade. Use dated smart-money
pivots as a DISCOVERY INSTRUMENT against our own atom stream: for each early pivot, join
backward — what was in the knowable atom stream in the weeks before their move? Which of OUR
signal families would have fired? Their moves become labeled events for validating which
families carry real information — "understand the why" made mechanical. F6 is a labeling
channel first, a signal second.

## The case bench (retrodiction targets, each with epoch + tier + what it tests)

**C1 — ABF substrate shortage (~2019-2022).** Ajinomoto Build-up Film bottleneck; Ibiden/
Shinko/AT&S capacity crunch; lead times exploding while niche press covered it quarters
before earnings confirmed. Tier-A/B rich (supplier prints, capex announcements, DigiTimes/
SemiAccurate-class coverage with wayback presence). TESTS: supplier-print divergence +
equipment/capacity lead (the causal pair) on their home ground. Cleanest first retrodiction.

**C2 — AMD chiplet bet in infancy (~2017-2019).** Zen/EPYC Naples->Rome; the MCM design
mocked then vindicated; AdoredTV's Rome leaks and SemiAccurate coverage ran YEARS ahead of
consensus — Daniil's own college watched it live. TESTS: expert-narrative lead + credit-
weighted expert-vs-consensus (can the credit loop distinguish the outlets that were RIGHT
from the ones that were loud?). Also the survivorship control case: outlets that called it
wrong must drag their credit down in the same window.

**C3 — Datacenter power crunch / portable turbines (~2023-2026).** AI buildout outrunning
grid interconnects; gas-turbine and mobile-generation repricing (GE Vernova/Siemens Energy
class). UNIVERSE EXTENSION: the methodology ported beyond semis to the AI-power complex —
same atoms, same registry, adjacent ground truth. TESTS: whether the pipeline generalizes
without semis-specific priors.

**C4 — Hormuz-class disruption (LNG + specialty gases/materials).** Geopolitical shock
propagation into fab inputs (energy costs for fabs; specialty gases: neon's 2022 Ukraine
precedent, helium, etch/depo gases). FEW-SAMPLE honesty: event studies, not families —
feeds the REGIME-BREAK taxonomy and the drift alarms more than the signal roster. Explicitly
not a backtestable family; it is break-condition material.

**C5 — The current DRAM crisis (live, 2025-2026).** HBM crowding conventional DRAM capacity;
price inflection. THE LIVE CASE: run F6 reverse-inference on who pivoted into memory names
early, when, and against what knowable signals — AND the archivist (P0b) captures the
narrative stream IN REAL TIME from today, making this the first case where Tier-C sources
become Tier-B by our own stamping. The case where the moat starts accruing.
