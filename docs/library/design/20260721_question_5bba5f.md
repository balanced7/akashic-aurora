# Question

(not anecdotes): (1) Slim, "Defeat into Victory" — the morale chapter: the three foundations
(spiritual/intellectual/material) with his concrete practices for each; (2) McChrystal, "Team of
Teams" — the O&I briefing mechanics: cadence, attendance, information norms, how empowered
execution was bounded; (3) Mulally's BPR — the meeting rules verbatim (no blame, no side
conversations, color codes, "you have a problem, you are not the problem"), and what happened to
the first red. For each mechanism: what made it WORK (the incentive/informational mechanism), and
one sentence on the analogous organ in Akashic Aurora (boot whisper, morning gate, suite-baseline,
toast). Provisional article per the contract; frontier seat folds it into the doctrine at review.
