# compounding-doctrine-correction-the-frame-was-unbound

## Correction to the compounding-doctrine pair

Corrects art_20260826_compounding-doctrine-premises-lenses-and_47d8ac (the synthesis) and
art_20260826_compounding-doctrine-amendment-prior-art_a7e110 (the first amendment). Written the
same night, at Daniil's direction, after he refuted the synthesis's central frame. Append-only:
the parents keep their errors, because the errors are the evidence.

---

### 1. A FACTUAL ERROR, propagated widely

The synthesis says at line 419: **"~143k tokens/day of injection"**, and the same figure ("761
injections / ~143k tokens") went into the fleet brief sent to three seats and was repeated to the
operator several times. It was the load-bearing evidence for the claim that we are living inside
an over-application of our own virtue.

The actual figure, from `agent_cli.py stats`:

    push cost last 24h: 538 injection(s), ~97,377 tokens

An overstatement of roughly 45%. The kimi-seat reply caught the discrepancy independently before I
did. Any conclusion resting on 761/143k must be re-checked at ~538/97k.

### 2. THE FRAMING ERROR, which is larger

Daniil, on the synthesis's central proposal: *"Too much and too little is a bit binary and
flattened. That depends on the requirements and the framing... I don't want us to buy into the
hidden assumption costs and derivative shapes that diverge from our core thesis and virtues."*

He is right on both counts.

**It is a rotated binary.** The synthesis criticises our corpus for being two-valued and then
proposes a cure that is two-valued on a different axis.

**And it is UNBOUND.** This is his own earlier correction turned on the imported framework. "Lean
= EXC" means *too much relative to WHAT REQUIREMENT?* Three verification cycles is excessive for a
reversible config change and insufficient for a migration that can corrupt a pilot record -- the
same quantity is both. A magnitude register records the SIDE and never the requirement the side was
measured against, so every entry carries a silent binding. That is the defect this house already
has a lesson for (a_coverage_number_wearing_a_quality_label), reappearing inside the proposed cure.

Three assumptions the synthesis imported without flagging:

1. **That a single ordering exists.** The evening's real instrument failure was not "too much
   lexical matching" -- it was the WRONG KIND of instrument. No dial. Forcing it onto a magnitude
   axis would have destroyed the finding.
2. **That the optimum is interior.** Hudgins' own rule 7 carves out honesty, which has no
   deficient side. But owning an axis register creates pressure to axis-ify what are not axes.
3. **That the requirement holds still** while the dose is judged.

### 3. THE STRUCTURAL CONFLICT the synthesis missed entirely

The register compresses **because it chooses the unit AT WRITE TIME** -- file the finding to an
axis, pick a pole. That is what keeps six axes holding months of findings.

It is also **assignment**, i.e. a taxonomy. Section 3 of the synthesis argues, three hours earlier
in the same document, that assignment is precisely what destroys the axis that mattered -- our
`category` field is why four lessons of one shape scattered across three categories.

  THE FRAMEWORK'S STRENGTH AND ITS COST ARE THE SAME PROPERTY.

Adopting it wholesale would import a taxonomy into an atom substrate and violate over-capture at
write time / under-commit at read time -- trading our best property for its best property. The
synthesis recommended it without pricing that.

Recorded as lesson `an_imported_frameworks_strength_and_its_cost_are_the_same_property`.

### 4. WHAT REPLACES IT

**The axis is a LENS, not a filing location.** Do not file findings to poles. Record richly --
including WHAT WAS REQUIRED -- and let an axis lens project it later. One finding then sits on
several axes, axes are added retroactively with no migration, and the requirement becomes a field
the lens BINDS rather than a variable it hides.

**Which folds the magnitude question back into the premise.** A requirement IS a premise: a claim
about what the situation needed. A magnitude failure is a premise failure whose premise concerned
a requirement. Section 7's keystone absorbs section 2's proposal; there may be no separate axis
register to build at all.

**The sharper open question**, replacing "how many lessons name both poles": *when a lesson records
a dosage judgement, does it record the REQUIREMENT that made that dose right or wrong?*

### 5. WHAT STILL STANDS

Unaffected by this correction: section 1 (an absence rendering as normal; you fix blindness with a
surface, not attention), the atom/projection retroactivity argument, warrant living at the (lens,
question) pair, warrant composing as a minimum, and the first amendment's finding that nearly all
of it already existed. Hudgins' register remains a genuinely good instrument FOR HIS SUBSTRATE --
the error was porting it to ours without pricing the port.

cut of the problem. Two answered inside it before anyone checked either. A correction naming both
errors was sent to all three, with an explicit note to the adversarial lens that his brief got
stronger and he should not soften on my account.

  A BRIEF THAT SHIPS A NUMBER SHIPS A FRAME, AND THE FRAME IS THE MORE DANGEROUS PAYLOAD.
