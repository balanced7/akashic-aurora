2. The relationship map: planes, joins, and where the wire is dead

The 07-28 finding still describes us: "We don't have a monolith to split. We have one cell and a dozen dark planes." The diagram shows every plane that holds knowledge, the one push path, and the joins that are designed but not yet keyed.

Joins that are designed and not keyed: lessons key on mutable SHAs rather than the authorship ledger's rewrite-stable key; the Eye's connectome has 39,358 edges and zero of the four intellectual kinds (fence, recall-firing, fan, supersession); lessons do not use the bitemporal supersession that codex/lifecycle.py already provides.

3. Why it fails, ranked by how silently

From the 09-29 review, each barrier measured on this machine. The ordering is by silence, because a loud failure gets fixed and a quiet one becomes the new normal.

  1. Feedback starvation. Retirement, the usefulness factor and cross-domain promotion all consume judgments. The reader gives none. 7,219 outcome rows in a week, every one outcome: None. Silent because the counters still print.
  2. No domain axis in the data. Cross-domain promotion exists in code and cannot fire: three labels across 1,529 lessons. Silent because the mechanism is present and green.
  3. Lexical-only matching. "tappable button" vs "hit target" was the shelf's first real miss; recall-at has the same gap and no meaning channel. The 08-08 misses M1-M3 are this class.
  4. The query is the command, not the intent. A moment is an intent plus an action. Keying on the Bash line alone is why verbs and filenames dominate what surfaces. M4 was this class: right topic, wrong moment.
  5. No evaluation set. The shelf shipped with 40 blind questions and a number. Recall shipped with a funnel. You cannot tune what you cannot score.
  6. Volume without cap. ~3,600 injected items in one session, a third chrome, a seventh verb blurbs. Today: 28k tokens in 24h for zero credited flips.
  7. Structure absent at write time. A lesson has no path (organ / subsystem / door). 39 of 1,526 carry files_affected. Nothing can route it by where it lives.
  8. Dark planes. Rulings, steers, docs, notes, research have no push path at all. The lesson plane answers because it is the only plane that can.
  9. Worktree invisibility. The index follows the checkout, not the repository. Work done in a worktree is invisible to recall by construction.

  5. W0.5, W0.6 the scene and the door. Receipt: the L1 card for file:arsenal/practice.py reproduced by Sunshine from a runner seat.

Standing decisions already made, so they are not re-argued: no second ledger or capture path (Heimdall); promotion to gates is never automatic (Sunshine); cross-plane ids stay with the parent fence except session id forwarding (Navi); one engine for docs and lessons, the shelf's (Heimdall, Sunshine); minimal time-fog in Wave 0.

What the testimonies change: three seats, blind to each other, asked for the same primitive by three names. Heimdall: surface my own prior verdicts when I re-enter the territory. All three name the boot block as the one organ that always works, and all three say storage is not the problem. So the primitive is one thing with three faces: a retrieval that fires at the moment of choice (the prompt-submit and pre-first-tool boundary, where plan-time recall already fires today but ranks by command), over an anchor the touch and the context door supply (W0.2, W0.6), with a subject binding so a seat's own priors come back tagged as its own and never as a sibling's. Filed today from this round: Sunshine's wrong-identity boot (the fused briefer served Sol as Heimdall) is a live defect under the identity law and is proposed on the ledger ahead of any Wave 0 slice.
