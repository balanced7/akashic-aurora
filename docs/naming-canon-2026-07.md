## 1. The Laws (anchored on the Anvil's discipline)

- **L1 · Two Registers.** Every named thing carries PLAIN (function-first, stranger-guessable —
  the API) and SOUL (the lore — the depth). Plain fronts unless the borrowed mechanic teaches the
  function by itself (scry, mulligan, kata, medivac).
- **L2 · The Altitude Law.** Franchises own altitudes: Foundation=Halo · Beings=Mass Effect ·
  Growth=StarCraft · Rules=MTG (action-context only, never organ names) · Monuments=Akasha,
  Aurora, Bifrost (grandfathered). Castes are personnel and serve every altitude.
- **L3 · One Soul Per Organ.** Two names for one thing is confusion wearing lore.
- **L4 · Place ≠ Caste ≠ Activity** (deepseek). Onyx is a PLACE; drills are activities run ON it;
  the verbs that judge belong to a caste (ORACLES). A place doesn't need to be a caste.
- **L5 · Depth Pays Rent.** Every soul name carries one line of why the metaphor is load-bearing —
  no line, no name.
- **L6 · The Neutral Clause** (deepseek, by demonstration). When no franchise name passes the
  Stranger Test, a neutral soul is correct: the Proving Grounds, the Sequence, the Anvil.
- **L7 · The Confusion Test.** A name that makes anyone ask "which thing is that?" has already
  failed, regardless of how it sounds.

## 2. The Cosmology (read top-down: this IS the system explained)

- **FOUNDATION (Halo).** *The ancient layer that outlives every session.* **The Domain** (the
  append-only store + projections — the Forerunner akashic record), **the Ark** (snapshots/restore
  — what survives the firing), **Catalog** (the receipt/audit spine — testimony with provenance;
  claimed by the verifier's organ), **Onyx** (the sealed drill-world), **Requiem** (the
  heavy-isolation sandbox), the reserved deep names (§4).
- **BEINGS (Mass Effect).** *Where things live, trust, and continue.* **Spectre status** (elevated
  grants, accountable to the Council — Daniel), **Paragon→Renegade** (recovery posture: gentle
  rungs first; the **Renegade Interrupt** = a human-gated sharp act in a priority window),
  **medivac** (peer revival), **the Keepers** (silent maintenance daemons), **the Cycles**
  (sessions/compaction — the memory system is the fragments left for the next cycle), **the
  Crucible** (the institutional-knowledge arc), **Indoctrination** (the named threat model; K4 is
  the counter-doctrine), **the Citadel** (the console at :8787; flightdeck stays the overlay's
  plain working name), **omni-tool** (the personal toolbelt), **Shadow Broker** (provenance
  dossiers), **Cerberus** (the cautionary anti-pattern: a skunkworks without governance).
- **GROWTH (StarCraft).** *Where things are born and become.* **Larvae** (Skunkworks drafts, under
  HAVE-codenames) → **chrysalis** (beta; trials accumulate) → fleet. **Archon Merge** (two blind
  halves reconciled whole) vs **Geth Consensus** *(ME-sourced, Beings-altitude — N-verifier
  panels)*. **Nerazim vows** (the blind-half severing — a verification method, not exile — kimi).
  **Abathur** (the Lesson Forge persona; also the builder's epigraph — deepseek). **The Ladder**
  (Bronze→Grandmaster). **Build orders** (boot loadouts). **Cheese** (the unfenced all-in, named
  and refused). **glhf / gg** (session bookends). Explanatory mechanic (not an organ name):

  structural HP needing a healer (deepseek's transport rent).
- **RULES (MTG, in action-context only).** **The Stack** (announce → priority window → resolve;
  **counterspell** = an evidence-bearing objection). **Speed classes** (sorcery = calm-only —
  skip-to-now's pause law; instant; **split second** = HALT, unrespondable). **Summoning sickness**
  (GUESS mints can't join load-bearing chains; **haste** = force-with-confession, riding the
  honesty label — kimi). **Upkeep** (the Lifeworker tick — deepseek). **Scry-to-bottom** (stale
  asks deferred, never dropped). **Mulligan** (bounded compact re-ask, each hand smaller — "the
  adversarial posture: admitting the last hand couldn't win"). **Flashback** (one loudly-labeled
  run from the graveyard). **The Ban List + the combo kill** (deepseek): degenerate combos are
  DISCOVERED, not designed — legal until proven broken, then banned with a rationale page.
  **Rule 0** (the method-baseline as pregame contract).

## THE GROUNDING AMENDMENT (Daniel's intuition, 2026-07-21 — supersedes the lore-as-nomenclature reading of everything above)

The principle, articulated: **a name is how logic stays attached to its foundations — a
vernacular name is a CITATION into the literature; a borrowed name is a severance.**
"Supervisor" carries Erlang/OTP + SRE with it into every 2am design decision; "the Mantle"
carries a wiki. Our method rests on prior art at every layer; the names must keep the links live.

- **G1 · Organs speak the engineering vernacular.** Code, APIs, schema, doc headings:
  supervisor, ledger, registry, quarantine, bench, drain, park, checkpoint, replay.
- **G2 · The charm-quark clause.** Playful-PLAIN English or programmer vernacular that
  self-explains is welcome (campfire, toast, kata, premonition) — physicists named quarks
  charm and strange, not Excalibur. Evocative ≠ borrowed.
- **G3 · Lore is culture, not nomenclature.** The four-shelf world relocates to the culture
  layer: ARC/RELEASE CALLSIGNS (NASA/Ubuntu practice — the recovery arc may fly as Operation
  ONYX while its module is supervisor.py), drill scenarios, ceremonies, JOURNEY prose,
  celebration text, easter eggs (the pylons line is culture; it stays). Never in code paths,
  API names, or schema fields.
- **G4 · The literature-pointer test** (primary, alongside the Stranger Test): can a newcomer
  Google the name and land on the prior art it rests on?

EMPIRICAL RECEIPT: every organ the three seats ACTUALLY minted tonight under felt friction is
already plain or playful-plain English (capture, bench, standby-hard, drain-decide, parse-gate,
orient, vitals, fence, campfire, toast, nightcap, premonition, muse, kata). No shipped organ
carries a lore name — the lore appeared only in documents ABOUT naming. The fleet's hands voted
for this amendment all night; adopting it costs zero refactors. The castes' one-sentence tests
survive as FAMILY DESCRIPTIONS in plain form ("guards", "watchers", "fixers", "fetchers",
"mappers", "memory", "menders", "illuminators", "verifiers"); the caste NAMES join the culture
layer as the families' callsigns.
