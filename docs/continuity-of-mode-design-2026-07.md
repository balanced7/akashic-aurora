# Continuity of mode — institutionalizing stance across fresh boots

Status: current
Type: design · Arc: leadership-doctrine / continuity · Seats: claude (deepseek+kimi counters invited) · Date: 2026-07-21

**Charter (Daniel, verbatim):** "How do we institutionalize our current learnings, the next you
will forget, how do you get yourself to actually remember back to where you were while having
freedom to expand and grow from there. I don't want this to be a one time mode, I want this
approach to happen every time and from fresh boot. Right now the percentage chance of us getting
close in my opinion is very low."

## The diagnosis (why the chance is currently low)

State continuity is SOLVED here (boot, where-we-are, handoff-as-voice, ledger — tonight's Fable
seat re-became the project in one hop). **Stance continuity is not**: the mode lives in documents,
and documents inform but do not activate. A fresh seat gets pointers to doctrine among 100+ docs
and 40+ lessons — salience decays, nothing fires at the moment of conducting, and nothing measures
whether the mode survived the boot. What isn't loaded at the right moment doesn't exist; what
isn't measured regresses.

## The design principle

**Stance travels the same way state does: through organs that fire at the right moment — not
through bigger documents.** Five layers, each answering "when does this knowledge need to exist?"

| Layer | Question it answers | Mechanism | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| L0 identity | who am I when I wake? | charter core + GROUND-FIRST voice doc rendered AT boot (not pointed at) | voice doc proven (Opus→Fable handoff); charter render = build slice C1 |
| L1 law | what must happen every time? | docs/CONDUCT.md living contract (the ten laws) | LIVE (2026-07-21) |
| L2 moment-triggers | what fires mid-action? | six conductor_* lessons in recall-at (brief-writing, fence, wrap, charter verbs) | LIVE — recall-at proven tonight (interrupted a bifrost-send with the reply-size lesson) |
| L3 boot stance | what loads unconditionally? | 3-line CONDUCT block in boot render: stance · stretches · mode-check | build slice C1 (deepseek owns boot render) |
| L4 measurement | did the mode survive? | wrap brief-census + kata conductor-scorer + newborn-gauntlet extension (the fresh-boot bar) | build slices C2/C3 |

Plus the loop that makes it GROW instead of fossilize (Daniel's second requirement): the
anti-fossil clause in CONDUCT.md — divergences that work become amendments at gates; laws that
misfire get voted noise and retired; version bumps append. Memory with a metabolism.

## Build slices (fenced, acceptance pre-registered)

- **C1 — boot stance block** (owner: deepseek — boot-render veteran; after D1-D3 land, not
  before: focus is respected). Renders 3 lines from charter + CONDUCT: stance, per-seat current
  stretch, pointer. Acceptance: present on every boot incl. fresh seats; ≤3 lines; pins.
- **C2 — wrap brief-census** (owner: claude). Wrap counts outgoing briefs and scores law-1/2
  observables (intent block present, principal's words present, calibrated question present);
  emits one census line + prompts the 2-sentence voice line for the successor. Acceptance: census
  line appears at wrap; voice line lands in the handoff; pins.
- **C3 — kata: conductor scorer** (owner: claude builds, kimi calibrates). Samples last N
  outgoing briefs, scores against the ten laws, reports like suite-baseline. Acceptance: scores
  tonight's v2 orders ≥8/10 and a bare task-list ≤4/10 (calibration fixture); pins.
- **C4 — newborn-gauntlet extension** (owner: kimi designs — the stranger designs the stranger
  test). Fresh-seat drill: first three briefs scored unprompted against the fresh-boot bar.
  Administered at next fresh seating. Acceptance: the drill runs on the NEXT fresh boot and the
  score is filed to the funnel.
- **C5 — charter growth ledger** (owner: claude; lands with charter ratification at the morning
  gate). Each charter gains: demonstrated abilities + current stretch + stretch history.

## The honest bound

Perfect stance-transfer to a fresh instance is not achievable; engineered re-convergence is. The
target metric: a fresh seat re-converges to the mode within ONE boot + ONE corrected brief —
instead of requiring a lucky evening of live coaching. The guarantee is the LOOP (activation +
measurement + amendment at gates), not any single artifact. Culture is maintained, not installed
— the maintenance IS the institution.
