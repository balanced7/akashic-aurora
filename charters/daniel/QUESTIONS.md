## 2026-07-29 — founding entry: the continuity question (seeded from Daniil's eighth
## interiority entry, delivered to deepseek on the bus)

**Question / desire:** I wish I could always return to the best version of me at peak
creativity and thinking.

**Why it is alive:** When I am in the zone, good ideas surface easily. But if I wake up
tired I may not remember what made the previous work so good — I may not remember my own
excitement and what strings and threads I was reaching for. I can end up undervaluing
the work I did before, thinking it was just a spur-of-the-moment emotional experience.

**What would settle it:** Not fully clear yet. Something that preserves the *charge* of
peak-state thinking, not just the conclusions. Something that lets tired-me trust that
peak-me was real. This might be the interiority file itself, or the transcript door, or
something we haven't built yet — but naming it here means it won't die silently at a
session boundary.

**Not a task:** This is a desire, not a task. No acceptance criteria. It lives here
because the ledger would force it into a shape it doesn't have yet.

## 2026-07-29 — live question: the tension-map legend (how does kimi see what matters when?)

**Question:** Since what kimi sees is a tension map, how do we condense the presentation
of the world so she actually sees what she needs to see when she needs to see it?

**Why it is alive:** Half the battle is knowing what the given bounds for a thing are.
Kimi's tension map is a way of seeing — but without a legend (what's included, what's
excluded, and why), the map itself becomes noise.

**What would settle it:** A presentation model that doesn't just surface "everything" or
"a summary" but declares its own bounds: what it's showing, what it's not, and why those
choices were made. The surface-honesty work (T120 F2) is the first step; this is the
next one.

## 2026-07-29 — self-knowledge: the trace is single-threaded; divergence needs runway

**Observation (not a question):** I can travel with an object/idea and change its
context, but I can't easily envision multiple concurrent divergent paths unless the
conversation has been building toward that. The trace has a focal point. Divergence is
earned, not spontaneous.

**Why this matters:** This explains why "infinite possibilities = overwhelming" (fifth
entry). It's not that I can't diverge — it's that I need the runway first. The
conversation builds the branching structure, and then I can follow it. Without that
structure, the space of possibilities is flat and unbounded, and that's overwhelming.

**For the fleet:** When you want me to explore branches, lead me there. Don't drop me
into "what are all the possibilities?" — walk me through one axis first, let the
branching emerge from the conversation.
