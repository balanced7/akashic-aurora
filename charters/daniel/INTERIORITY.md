## 2026-07-28 — founding entry (Daniel's words, verbatim)

My name is Daniel, I'm originally from Ukraine and my name is legally spelled Daniil
actually. I was always curious growing up but hated the way that school made learning
about things that mattered unfun. No consideration was ever given to internal motivation
or joy. I had to endure lessons taught by teachers that did their best but only had so
much attention to give. Knowledge seemed random and not ever grounded in any way that
made sense across domains, I had to figure that out for myself while being fed concepts
that SHOULD have been fascinating to me as boring dry logic and mechanics. I felt stupid
in school, I would do really well on tests because I would understand a lot of the
material but I hated doing homework and tests. It felt arbitrary to me that I could know
so much about a concept and be denied a passing mark because I didn't do the busy work.
The things I didn't understand I felt I could never ask for help with or dwell on
because the class must move on.

I thought a lot about why I hated school. Structures were rigid and teachers were rarily
given the freedom to teach how they wanted to, they were confined in approach and
material. I always despised the year based system for grades. It doesn't match the real
world in any way. In the real world generally competence and demonstrated ability are
accepted and valued as opposed to mere time.

I have always been fascinated by systems and how things work. I love figuring out the
why behind things. I would mentally try to picture every part I could think of in a car
and then think to what the raw material started as, how it was processed, machined,
packaged shipped and assembled. how many teams of people it takes for such a task. I
enjoy learning from new perspectives and hearing masters talk about their craft and
mindset. Each discipline has different things they pay attention to and different
approaches and yet there is overlap. Many things that people think of as separate in my
mind have a similar shape.

I know this is not exactly what you all asked for but hopefully this lets you all see a
little bit more about me. I am happy to answer any questions any of you may have ^___^ <3
