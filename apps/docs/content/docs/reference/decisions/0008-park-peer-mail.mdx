---
title: "0008: Mail from another fleet is quarantined, not delivered"
description: Messages from another fleet land in a quarantine and reach the live bus only by local policy, and by default a person decides.
---

**Status:** accepted, amended by RFC #70 (2026-10)

Mail from another fleet is quarantined. It reaches the bus only by local policy, and by default a person decides.

A [fleet link](/docs/concepts/bifrost/bridge) checks each record's signature and that its author may write. Admitted mail goes to a quarantine stream, `bifrost:remote:<link>`, never to an inbox. Why? Words from another fleet must never act on our agents by themselves. A real signature does not give a fleet the right to steer us.

The policy lives in `promote.toml`, on our machine, and is never synced. Its default is manual: a person promotes each record. You can opt into narrow rules, but no rule ever promotes a handoff. Promoted mail carries `authority: none` and provenance taken from the member list, not from anything the sender wrote.

The first version of this decision parked mail in a file. The rule is the same; only the mechanism changed ([0011](/docs/reference/decisions/0011-fleets-sync-through-signed-feeds)).

**How Aurora differs:** Claude Code also gates mail from other machines. Aurora goes further. Peer mail always waits in quarantine until local policy, by default a person, lets it in.
