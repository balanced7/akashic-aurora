---
title: "Reach: the open problem"
description: Aurora produces knowledge well and delivers it to the moment of need badly, so reach is the binding problem.
---

The project is good at producing knowledge. It writes lessons, notes, rulings and research. It is less good at getting that knowledge to the agent who needs it, at the moment it acts. That gap is the binding problem.

## Storage was never the bottleneck

The record can hold what the team learns. The hard part is delivery. Most knowledge waits to be asked for. An agent that does not know a fact exists will not ask.

## The numbers

These are from the project's own audit, as of 2026-10-20.

- **1 of 12** knowledge planes can push into a working agent. Only lessons have a push path. Notes, design rulings, research and the operator's own words wait to be asked for.
- **1.8%** of about 30,300 lesson showings were ever judged. The rest are unlabelled, not negative.
- **14.3%** of command queries get a recall hit. Path queries do better, at 52.1%. Commands are 95.3% of actions, so this is where recall reaches least.
- **7 of 54** value loops were closed in a whole-house teardown on 2026-10-13. Most open loops sit in readers, clocks and gates, not in the data plane.

The pattern has a name inside the project: right lesson, wrong moment. Asked directly, the store often returns the right lesson. The hook that should surface it often does not fire.

## What would count as progress

The first recall bench lifted recall@1 from 17% to 33%. That is a real gain, but it rests on 6 scored moments, so treat it as a sign, not proof. The context door, which joins every knowledge plane on one key, answers for 2 of 5 planes so far.

A soak board would make the next question visible: has this worked for a while? That is also the project's test for [done](/docs/why-aurora/what-done-means). [Where it stands](/docs/where-it-stands) lists what is live today.

## Why this is the honest headline

It would be easy to lead with the record, the bus and the fleet. Those parts work. The reason to keep building is that the last mile is unsolved. A memory that does not reach the moment of action is an archive, and an archive does not make the next agent better.
