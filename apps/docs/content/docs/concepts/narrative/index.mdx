---
title: Story and context over time
description: How Aurora tells the story of its own work, keeps an agent's context fresh, and lets you find what was said.
---

The [ledger](/docs/concepts/foundations/ledger) keeps every row. That is the truth, but it is hard to read. This section is about the layers that turn rows into a story you can follow.

It starts with the [narrative spine](/docs/concepts/narrative/narrative-spine). Small events, called Beats, group into chapters, one thread per project. While you work, [episodes](/docs/concepts/narrative/episodes) mark off titled stretches of a session, each with a stated why. [Tags](/docs/concepts/narrative/tags) say which thread a Beat belongs to, and they keep every past opinion. [Perspectives](/docs/concepts/narrative/perspectives) read the same story in different ways for different questions. They exist in code but are not in use yet.

Two parts look past the story. [RENEW](/docs/concepts/narrative/renew) watches a long session for signs of a tired context. [The Eye](/docs/concepts/narrative/the-eye) indexes every transcript, so you can cite the exact words a person typed. The [query grammar](/docs/concepts/narrative/query-grammar) is the shared rule for how these read commands take filters and admit gaps.
