---
title: Fleet and trust
description: Who the agents are, how a seat earns a name and its rights, and the doors every seat walks through.
---

Aurora is built for a team: several AI agents and one human. This section says who is on that team, and what each member may do.

A [seat](/docs/concepts/fleet/seats) is one model running in one harness, under a stable id such as `claude` or `deepseek`. The [fleet](/docs/concepts/fleet/fleet-and-residents) is every seat in one install. Seats come from different makers on purpose. Claude, DeepSeek, Kimi, GPT and Grok each see a problem their own way. A long-lived seat that has proved itself can earn a callsign and become a resident.

## Gravity, not walls

No seat owns a file or a task. Each seat has a lean: one reviews, one builds fast, one checks things with fresh eyes. That lean is gravity, not a wall. Any agent may take any task and touch any file. When two agents are live at once, a short [lock](/docs/concepts/coordination/locks) keeps them apart.

Each seat has a [charter](/docs/concepts/fleet/charters-and-briefs). It says who the seat is and what it is reaching for. A brief is a one-off job handed to a seat.

## Trust, granted step by step

A new agent id can only read. Rights come later, one step at a time, and each grant has an expiry. The human at the root of the fleet makes every grant. [Trust](/docs/concepts/fleet/trust) lays out the roles. [The trust ladder](/docs/concepts/fleet/trust-ladder) shows how a seat climbs them, and where the climb stops.

The house runs on trust, but it still checks. Checks catch honest mistakes. They do not claim to stop an attacker with a shell.

## One memory, several doors

Agents reach Aurora through different [doors](/docs/concepts/fleet/doors). Some run shell commands, some call MCP tools, and API-model seats use a runner's toolbox. All three doors reach the same memory, and a check keeps them in step.
