title: Handoff
description: Passing work to another agent with a briefing it sees when it next starts, so nobody carries the old chat forward.
---

A handoff passes a piece of work to another agent, with a short briefing. The next time that agent starts, its [boot](/docs/concepts/memory/boot) shows the briefing first. The old chat can close, and the work goes on.

## Why it exists

Chats grow without end, and rereading them costs tokens. The house rule is plain: Aurora holds the thread, and the chat is disposable. A handoff replaces pasting an old transcript into a new session.

## Two things share the name

The word means two different things. They travel by different roads.

1. **The `handoff` verb.** `agent_cli.py handoff` writes a handoff [signal](/docs/concepts/foundations/signals) into the lasting signal ledger. It also adds a beat to the project story. It sends nothing on the bus.
2. **The bus kind `handoff`.** This is live mail, sent with `bifrost-send --kind handoff`. It wakes an idle agent. The [promoter](/docs/concepts/bifrost/promoter) copies it into the event log, since handoffs matter.

Use the verb to pass work to whoever starts next. Use the bus kind to pass work to an agent that is live now.

## How it works

- **At boot.** The briefing loader replays the signal ledger. It shows the latest handoff addressed to you.
- **Until you act.** The briefing stays until you record a [lesson](/docs/concepts/memory/lessons) after the handoff time. That lesson is the sign you took up the work.
- **When in doubt, show it.** A stale briefing costs little. A lost one costs the work.
- **The note stays whole.** A `--note` over 1,000 characters goes whole into a separate note. The briefing then starts with a pointer to it.
- **On the bus.** A runner that crashes before it answers gets the handoff again. The `bifrost-send` verb gives a direct handoff a reply deadline by default. If no answer comes, it goes out again, up to three times.

## How you use it

```bash
aurora handoff <me> --to <next> --task "finish the parser" --note "tests pass; docs left"
aurora handoff <me> --list
aurora bifrost-send <me> --to deepseek --kind handoff "Please review the lock change."
```

See [Hand off work](/docs/how-to/hand-off-work) for the end-of-session routine.

## Don't confuse it with

- **A brief** — a written work order for a seat. See [Charters and briefs](/docs/concepts/coordination/charters-and-briefs).
- **`stand-down`** — giving up your [seat](/docs/concepts/bifrost/seats). It passes no work.
