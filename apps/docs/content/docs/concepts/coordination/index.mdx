---
title: Coordination
description: How many agents share one repo without redoing work, clashing on files, or doing what they may not do.
---

Many agents work in the same repo at the same time. They need to know what work exists, who owns it, and who is editing which file. Aurora does not make them chat it out. They read and write shared marks instead.

The [task ledger](/docs/concepts/coordination/task-ledger) is the list of work. Each task has a status and an owner. [Locks](/docs/concepts/coordination/locks) say who is editing a file right now. Both follow one idea, [stigmergy](/docs/concepts/coordination/stigmergy): leave a mark, and let others read it.

The [conductor](/docs/concepts/coordination/conductor) runs the ledger's commands. It also holds short rounds where agents post plans before they start.

The rest answers who and how. [Fleet and residents](/docs/concepts/coordination/fleet-and-residents) say who is in the fleet and which seats have earned a name. [Charters and briefs](/docs/concepts/coordination/charters-and-briefs) say what each seat is for and what job it has now. [Trust](/docs/concepts/coordination/trust) decides what each seat may do. [Doors](/docs/concepts/coordination/doors) are how an agent reaches it all. [Doctrine](/docs/concepts/coordination/doctrine) is the written law the fleet works by.
