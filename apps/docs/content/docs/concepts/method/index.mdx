---
title: The method
description: The habits for asking, checking and shipping work here, and the guards that back the most important ones.
---

These pages show how we ask, check and ship work. Each habit came from a real mistake. Scripts enforce the most important ones.

The story runs in order. You start with a question. You [ask](/docs/concepts/method/ask-and-fan) a helper model, or fan the question out to several at once. When answers come back, you keep [every distinct finding](/docs/concepts/method/union-not-consensus), not only the ones they agree on. Every claim must [cite its proof or admit the gap](/docs/concepts/method/cite-or-confess).

For work that could hurt if wrong, two agents review it apart. Then a third rules on where they differ. That is a [fence review](/docs/concepts/method/fence-review). A task closes as done only with a commit and a record of the check. That is [verification](/docs/concepts/method/verification).

Two last checks keep the code honest. Code that exists but never runs counts as [built, not wired](/docs/concepts/method/built-not-wired). And rules that matter live in scripts, not in good intentions. That is [guards over discipline](/docs/concepts/method/guards-over-discipline). The nine [principles](/docs/concepts/method/principles) sit under all of it.
