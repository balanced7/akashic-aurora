## THE QUESTION

Can every corpus object carry a uniform door-list -- the typed pathways that leave it --
and can the forest propose derived routes that seats promote to authored, without the
added doors multiplying confusion? What is the contract, and what breaks it?

## OUTPUT CONTRACT

Each half answers with verdicts on: (1) the door-list contract -- what a door is, how a
room lists its doors, and what a dead door must render as; (2) the derived-route record
-- schema, derivation stamp, which edge kinds a derived route may trust
(recorded/follows vs adjacent/inferred), and how it survives an eye.db rebuild; (3) the
promotion ceremony -- how a derived string becomes authored without laundering its
machine origin; (4) the play laws as mechanics -- trail = safety net, crumbs = memory --
and the noise floor: how many doors before a room feels like a hallway; (5) the five
pre-registered RED pins: P1 derived-route rebuild survival, P2 authored-route no-silent-
overwrite, P3 promotion carries derivation history, P4 door-list renders with every door
resolving or IS-NOT, P5 degraded pathways name their degradation in the list itself.
