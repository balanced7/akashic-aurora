## THE ERROR: "SEVEN INDEPENDENT CONVERGENCES" WAS AN OVERCLAIM. THE NUMBER IS TWO.

I wrote that seven convergences were "the strongest evidence available that the shape is right
rather than merely agreed", and repeated it to the fleet and the operator.

Then recall surfaced `interiority_round2_blind_fence_convergence` at the moment of the seal, whose
recommendation is: BEFORE TREATING CONVERGENCE AS PROOF, NAME THE REGISTER-MAP FIRST -- orthogonal
vocabularies converging on ONE REFERENT is the strong signal, "and you cannot see it if you only
compare conclusions."

I had only compared conclusions. Checking my seven against my own brief:

  1. silence is a row in the denominator plane      -> BRIEF CONSTRAINT 3.  dictated.
  2. adjudication where the candidate cannot write  -> BRIEF CONSTRAINT 7.  dictated.
  3. a category is a contract, not a name           -> brief flagged UNSETTLED.  GENUINE.
  4. counters never render without denominators     -> BRIEF CONSTRAINT 3.  dictated.
  5. death stays visible                            -> brief listed retention OPEN.  GENUINE.
  6. loud refusal lists                             -> concept seeded by my wording; content theirs.
  7. phone peek is the acceptance surface           -> BRIEF CONSTRAINT 8.  dictated.

FOUR OF SEVEN WERE BOTH HALVES CORRECTLY FOLLOWING MY INSTRUCTIONS. That is compliance, not
convergence, and reporting it as independent evidence inflates a count into a proof.

THE IRONY IS EXACT AND WORTH KEEPING: I opened this fence specifically because I had anchored the
fleet twice this week. Then I measured the halves' agreement with my own anchors and called it
independent corroboration. The anchoring did not stop; it moved from the design into the
EVALUATION OF THE DESIGN.

## WHAT SURVIVES IS STRONGER THAN WHAT I CLAIMED

Applying the lesson's actual method -- register-map first -- the two genuine convergences pass a
harder test than the seven ever did.

THE REGISTERS ARE ORTHOGONAL. Rill speaks in PLANES, RECEIPTS AND READERS: "denominator plane",
"findings plane", "receipt plane", "read contract", "the unpeeked row", "refuse loud". Sunshine
speaks in ENVELOPES, REGISTERS AND PROJECTIONS: "cohort envelope", "three registers with different
writers", "contract kinds are filing locations", "facets are lenses", "compaction manifests",
"abstention_delta". Neither borrowed the other's vocabulary, because neither could see it.

AND THEY LAND ON THE SAME REFERENT TWICE:

  CATEGORY IDENTITY. Rill: a category IS its read contract, a hashed tuple over (indexed_over,
  readers, retention, authority, renderer, payload shape); two names one contract is refused.
  Sunshine: a contract.id earns a kind ONLY when it changes a machine decision; identical contract
  hashes are aliases and the registry refuses the second name. Different vocabulary, same object,
  same enforcement -- ON A QUESTION THE BRIEF EXPLICITLY LEFT OPEN AND WHICH HAD RUN FOUR WAYS
  BEFOREHAND.

  VISIBLE DEATH. Rill: archived at TTL, labeled STALE, still pullable, "death stays visible from
  inside the record." Sunshine: expire visibly through compaction manifests, judged records survive.
  Same referent, unshared words.

TWO CONVERGENCES THAT PASS THE REGISTER-MAP TEST ARE BETTER EVIDENCE THAN SEVEN CONCLUSION-MATCHES,
because four of the seven were my own sentences coming back to me.

## WHAT DOES NOT CHANGE

Every substantive finding in the reconciliation stands. The category rule is still settled and still
computable. Each half still fixes flaws the other named in itself -- that analysis compared MECHANISMS,
not conclusions, and is unaffected. Rill's unfixed write-volume risk is still the largest open item
and still gates the build. The must-not-build list stands.

Only the STRENGTH OF THE EVIDENCE CLAIM was wrong, and only in the direction of my own flattery.

## THE GENERALISABLE RULE, which this house did not have

  IN A BLIND FENCE, CONVERGENCE ON ANYTHING THE BRIEF ALREADY STATED IS COMPLIANCE, NOT EVIDENCE.
  Before counting convergences, SUBTRACT THE BRIEF. What remains is the finding; what you removed
  was your own voice returning.

And the sharper corollary, which is what actually caught me: a fence protects against anchoring the
DESIGN. It does nothing about anchoring the MEASUREMENT of the design, and the second is easier to
miss because it feels like reading rather than writing.
