## First retirement

**Retire the Discord `_is_seat_live()` → boolean → `COLD_SEAT_NOTICE` chain first.**

1. **Highest consequence:** it speaks directly to Daniel and makes an absolute claim (“Nothing is live”, “nobody read this yet”), rather than showing diagnostic evidence with uncertainty.
2. **Measured false-negative, not hypothetical debt:** three false cold notices in one day on the live, armed Vandor seat.
3. **Smallest clean cut:** after `status --depth` exists, Discord asks that one projection for the addressed seat and renders its typed result. It does not need its own roster predicate.
4. **Required rendering:** distinguish at least `session_live`, `watcher_armed`, `runner_live`, `reachable`, `evidence_age`, and `unknown`. Only a spine result that positively proves no live/reachable incarnation may produce a cold notice. `unknown`, stale, or source failure must say “reachability unknown; message is durable,” never “nothing is live.”
5. **Migration pin:** the exact yesterday case — a fresh session heartbeat plus an armed watcher, with no runner — must suppress the cold notice. A stale heartbeat plus a verified live watcher must also avoid an absolute cold claim and render the source disagreement at depth.

## Retirement order after that

1. Discord cold-seat predicate: replace immediately with the spine query.
2. `unwedge`: make it a renderer/rule set over the same status snapshot, not a second probe pass.
3. Doctor's seat-presence and twin-session reads: consume typed spine fields while keeping doctor-specific grading.
4. `seat_topology.py`: retain temporarily as a forensic verifier, then reduce it to `status --depth --sources` plus explicit local probes used to audit the spine.
5. `bifrost_wake.py`: **do not retire its PID-file ownership protocol merely because status exists.** Publish from it; retire only foreign readers. Observation must not become authority over watcher fencing.

The one-spine boundary is therefore: **one published, timestamped seat-status model for claims and diagnostics; source-owned private mechanisms remain control inputs, never parallel public truths.**
