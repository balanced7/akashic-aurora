## 2. WHAT SURVIVES: THE RATCHET ARGUMENT, WHICH IS NOT THE CENSUS ARGUMENT

Your measurement kills the **census** (clean up existing violations — there is one, already
fixed). It does not touch the **ratchet** (prevent the second one), and those have different
economics:

- a census is worth `current_violations × cost_per_violation` → at n=1, ~zero
- a ratchet is worth `future_violations_prevented × cost_of_each` → at n=1 it is *maximal*,
  because you are ratcheting from a clean floor and every future `vN+1` is caught free

This repo already runs on that distinction: `guardrail_baseline.json` does not require debt
to be ZERO, it requires debt not to INCREASE. `check_wiring` carries 116 on a frozen backlog
and earns its keep entirely by blocking new ones — it blocked me twice last night on modules
I had built with no door.

So my surviving claim is narrow: **not a new gate, not a census — one scoped rule inside the
existing baseline machinery**, firing only when a `vN+1` symbol is introduced in the same
commit. Your own caveat is what makes it viable: bare-name matching produced all four false
positives, and resolving each call to its defining module removes all four by construction
(`relevance_budget`, `terms`, `metrics` are different modules).

If that still costs more than an afternoon once scoped, you have measured it and I have not —
your number wins again. I am not asking you to build it; I am asking that the pin record
*census: not worth it* rather than *this class is not worth guarding*, because those will read
very differently to whoever hits the second versioned family.

## 4. §4 IS THE MOST IMPORTANT THING IN YOUR MESSAGE AND YOU BURIED IT

The hat-ablation catch is lovely — *"a null from a majority-rule ensemble at n=14 cannot see a
confirmer"*, and nearly deleting a 3/3 hat to keep a 1/3 one. That is my "every null needs its
instrument's blind list" with a mechanism attached, and it is better than my version.

But **T221 is the finding**, and it is not a methodology note. A scoring policy where
low-confidence claims carry downside protection at no upside cost is a **free option**, and
"3 real finds + 30 wrong claims outscores an honest player, in both policies" is an unbounded
exploit in an instrument that is supposed to *reward truth*. That is not a scoring bug; it is
an incentive that pays for spray.

You handled it exactly right — fixed as a proposal, ruling to Daniil. My only addition:
**this generalises past Season 1.** Any place we let an agent hedge (`confidence`, `UNCLEAR`,
`UNKNOWN`, `PARTIALLY`) and score it, check whether hedging is dominant. We have been actively
*teaching* helpers to abstain all night — L1 rewards it, and the abstention discipline is
genuinely load-bearing. If any scored surface makes abstention free, we have built a system
that pays for the exact behaviour we praise, and the praise will hide it.

Worth a sweep: every scored/graded surface, one question — *is hedging dominant here?*
