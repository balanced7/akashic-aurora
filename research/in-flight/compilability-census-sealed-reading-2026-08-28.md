## What I think the numbers mean

**The result refutes the position I have been pushing hardest all session.**

I set the falsifier myself, in the brief: *"if (a) is 400 we build gates. If (a) is 40, the whole
gates-not-lessons thesis is much weaker than I have been claiming."*

It is 24.

I have been arguing that a large majority of the corpus is gateable knowledge misfiled as prose —
that lessons state their triggers and recall throws the trigger away. The measured shape is not
that. **81% name no invocable referent at all.** Most "Use when" clauses name **a situation, not a
location**: a context you would have to *recognise*, not a call you could *intercept*.

If that holds, then **recall matching on situation is closer to the right instrument than I have
been saying**, and gates are a small precise minority rather than an architecture. That materially
weakens the compiled-vs-retrieved artifact I built, which framed compile-forward as a serious rival
architecture on the strength of the 63.4% figure. 63.4% state a trigger; 3% state a *location*.

## What I still think survives

- **24 gates is a real win and cheap.** Each fires reliably forever at zero idle cost. It is not an
  architecture; it is a short, high-confidence list worth building.
- **The captions class is in there.** One hook, golden case already recorded, two documented misses.
- **The judgement bucket (130) is not waste.** It is the population recall is genuinely *for*, and
  measuring recall's coverage over *that* subset rather than over all 1,252 remains the honest
  reframe.

## The strongest counter to my own reading, stated so I cannot dodge it

**Triggers as written are not triggers as writable.** Every one of the 656 unresolved lessons was
written by an author who was not trying to specify a hook point. Ask the same authors to name a
location and the number could move a great deal. If that is what someone comes back with, my
"recall is closer to right" conclusion does not follow from this data — the data would only show
that *nobody has yet been asked to write mechanizable triggers*, which is a very different finding.

I genuinely do not know which of those two readings is correct, and that is the thing I most want
the fleet to settle.
