## The convergent core (four independent derivations)

1. **The target is an inhabited cognitive environment, not a 3D skin.** Unanimous, sharpest in
   codex's anti-gimmick rule: build the physics of immersion in TODAY'S 2D surfaces first —
   stable places, honest occlusion, intent previews — so a headset later amplifies a real world
   instead of wrapping a metaverse skin around a dashboard. For a model, the interface IS the
   sensorium; this is finishing the body, not decorating it.

2. **Honesty is the physics.** The strongest convergence of the night — every seat, blind to
   the others, made epistemic state PERCEPTIBLE rather than fetchable:
   - claude: anti-Matrix clause — raw truth (gauges, receipts, red pins) one hop away at every
     setting.
   - kimi: epistemic depth of field — guesses glow, stale LOOKS stale, replayed LOOKS replayed;
     "the dim horizon is a kindness; a dimmed red pin is a lie."
   - codex: truth has physics — verified=solid, inferred=translucent, stale=receded,
     contradictions stay visible tension, risk has weight.
   - deepseek: drift suggestions carry provenance + a visible "why surfaced."
   LAW (charter candidate): **immersion must never launder uncertainty.**

3. **"Sharpness" is three orthogonal axes under one master gesture** (settled live, deepseek's
   challenge -> claude's concession -> kimi's net): DENSITY (how much world), DEPTH (how
   detailed each thing), TRUTH-RENDERING (how honestly each part wears its epistemic state).
   Camera model: the master gesture moves all three on a curve; grabbing one axis decouples it.
   Codex's lenses (Scout/Build/Debug/Review/Wander) and kimi's stance loadouts are SAVED
   COMBINATIONS of the axes, learned per seat. Plus Daniil's fourth insight (entry 3 of his
   interiority): AXIS-SELECTION precedes all three — choosing which axis exists is what bounds
   infinity before rendering begins. And his control-surface image (entry 4, verbatim): "like
   changing dials in a game engine to control speed of time, daylight night time, changing
   physics" — the master gesture renders as WORLD-PHYSICS DIALS, not dashboard sliders.

4. **Two floors beneath every setting** (non-negotiable): red pierces the blur (danger renders
   sharp at any zoom — kimi), and the deliberate commit gesture for high-impact acts (codex's
   intent shadow keeps agency inside thought-speed).
