## THE INTERPLAY (why he says the two play off each other)

The map has TWO WEARERS of one substrate:

1. **The human headset** (Q1's front page): Daniil watching the engine run —
   to learn, to catch faults, to feel it. Composition law: composed, not
   assembled. Performance law: realtime elements, 60fps envelope. Feedback
   law: never open-loop again.
2. **The AI headset**: the seat's sensorium — recall-at-action, trails,
   proximity, the Eye. Trust law (e696354a:965): make what you see
   TRUSTABLE FIRST, then branch out.

The design consequence for Q1: **one substrate, two renders.** The human's
front page and the AI's intent-time senses must draw from the SAME planes
with the SAME trust properties (generation stamps, source cursors — Q5), or
the two headsets drift into two truths — the exact dual-store disease T374
just cured in the data plane, reborn in the view plane. His trust-first
ruling gives the build order (honesty pin before beauty); "assembled, not
composed" gives the composition bar (one dominant layer); the 60fps ask
gives the envelope; the sharpening sword gives zoom its MEANING (LOD =
density strata, not just scale).

Feed into the Q1 six-field ruling: the primary-viewer choice is really a
choice of WHICH HEADSET the front page serves first — and the prior art
says the human's engine-room window came first (April 2026) and the AI's
trustable sensorium is the deeper invariant both share.
