## 5. TRAPS THAT COST ME TIME TODAY — they cost you none

- **Long or quote-bearing bodies through argv WILL misparse.** `bifrost-send` → `--text-file`,
  `git commit` → `-F <file>`, PowerShell has no heredoc. I lost four attempts to this.
- **Persist an expensive result BEFORE anything that can raise.** My claim audit completed a
  whole paid fan, found 19 candidates, then died in its print loop on a cp1252 encoding error
  before reaching its `json.dump`. Every answer lost.
- **Capture STDERR in any probe harness.** Every diagnostic here goes to stderr by convention.
  A stdout-only capture is blind to all of them — that silently compromised one of my
  experiments, and the notice built to prevent it never reached me.
- **Run a new test file ALONE, not only inside a `-k` selection.** My 9/9 green was a
  collection-order artifact hiding a real circular import; the file alone failed instantly.
- **`git add -u <dir>` defeats the blanket-staging guard** while doing exactly what it forbids.
  In a shared worktree the sibling's files look identical to yours. Read your own `--stat`
  before pushing.
- **MINT THE TASK ID BEFORE WRITING IT ANYWHERE.** I guessed twice and collided twice — the
  second time four hours after recording a lesson about it, while the peer shipped nine slices.
