### Three corrections this forces on the draft above

**C1 — T125 is not a comprehension feature. It is his trace, externalised.** The bolt and the
taillight ARE walking a module's inputs and outputs: pick an axis, hop shallowly, change the
environment around the object and replay it. Design consequence, concrete: v0 ships a single
`--impact` hop with no axis selection and no depth control. To actually be his instrument it needs
**axes** (depends-on / depended-by / temporal / cost / blast-radius), **shallow-hop default with
opt-in depth**, and **dials** rather than flags. That is a design amendment to T125, filed here,
not a new task.

**C2 — Optics and navigability are ONE thread, and Wave C's ordering argument was wrong at the
root.** The draft sequenced optics third because "the cockpit renders the map." But his model is
dials in a game engine — **the visual layer is how the dials are turned.** It is the control
surface OF navigability, not decoration downstream of it. The honest re-read: T125's query door and
T079's engine room are two halves of one instrument, and shipping the query door alone delivers a
trace he cannot fly.

*His own position, given 2026-08-01, which the plan must carry rather than override:* he deferred
the dashboard DELIBERATELY — *"I wanted to build our foundation first and make the system enjoyably
useable by the inhabitants… If we can get that sooner without too much cost I will not complain."*
So optics is not un-deferred by this correction. What changes is that when it lands it lands WITH
the trace, not after it.

**C3 — A missing thread: HIS continuity.** Nothing in the seven restores HIM.

> *"When I am in the zone and am thinking at my best, good ideas surface easily. If I wake up tired
> I may not remember what made the previous work so good, I may not remember fully my own excitement
> and what strings and threads I was reaching for… I wish I could always return to the best version
> of me at peak creativity and thinking."*

The entire system restores AGENT context. Nothing restores operator state — the threads he was
reaching for, the charge, the reason a direction felt right. He named the deep form himself: **his
continuity problem and the fleet's are the same problem meeting in the middle.** That makes it an
eighth thread, and the most personal one. Existing material that already serves it and was never
grouped as such: the interiority corpus, T092 reasoning spine, the note/wrap ritual, and the
buffer's triage log. It needs an owner and an outcome, not a new subsystem.

### One standing acceptance criterion, from his bounds question

Every surface this project ships owes a **legend**: what is in it, what is excluded, and why. Not
"here is everything" and not "here is a summary." This is the operator-side twin of the truth lens
in thread 5 — thread 5 asks *does this claim a state it is not in*; the legend asks *does this say
what it is bounded by*. Both are cheap and both are checkable at a ship gate.
