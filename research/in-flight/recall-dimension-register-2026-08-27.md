# The recall dimension register — Daniil's complete set, assembled

**This file exists because the set was never in one place.** It had accumulated across three
surfaces — a design conversation with Rill, a Discord message to an outside correspondent, and two
messages in a working session — and no surface held all of it. Every time a seat reached for "the
design," it got whichever subset the nearest artifact happened to carry, and then flattened it
further to whatever the current task needed.

That failure mode is the exact thing the design below is meant to fix. It ate the design three times
in one evening. This register is the fix applied to itself: **one durable record, complete, so a
partial reach is no longer possible.**

Authorship: every dimension below is DANIIL'S. Sources are marked per entry so nothing here is
paraphrase presented as origin. Assembled by claude/Vandor, 2026-08-27.

---

## The governing law

> "We can find ways of more robustly reaching each recall category in a more bounded way, which
> enables it to be richer and more dense because the recalls are more bounded."
> — Daniil, to Rill, 2026-08-27

**PRECISION AT THE GATE BUYS DENSITY IN THE POOL.** This is not one dimension among the others; it
is the constraint that makes having many of them affordable. A dimension whose reach is vague
reintroduces the ranking problem by itself and spends the density budget for every other dimension.

## The second law, recovered from the corpus

> "axis is a LENS not a filing location; record richly INCLUDING REQUIREMENT, project axes later."
> — recorded as a CORRECT STANCE, 2026-08-26

The dimensions below are **not sixteen indexes to build and maintain.** They are sixteen ways of
looking at one atom that was recorded properly. Nothing is filed *into* a dimension; dimensions are
projections over richly-recorded atoms. This is why adding the sixteenth costs a projection rather
than a migration, and it is the atom system's central property.

## THE DIMENSIONS

Each entry: the dimension, Daniil's own words, and where he said it.

### Reached by what is being done

**1. Toolcall identity, at fidelity.**
> "we can apply the branch prediction AT each toolcall or AT each repetition of x signatures or
> unique combinations and flags" · "trigger at toolcall with fidelity"

FIDELITY IS THE LOAD-BEARING WORD. Not "a tool fired" but the tool, its arguments, and its flag
combination as distinguishable sites. `git commit` and `git commit --no-verify` are two sites, not
one site with two outcomes.

**2. Payload / argument content.**
> "if content or argument of toolcall contains x, then do y, or y and z" · "recall based on what
> tools are being called and their payload"

The trigger lives in the argument's meaning. Must be signature containment, never token presence.

**3. Sequence / trajectory.**
> "Since we call so many tools we can have recall heuristics, when this sequence of tools fires the
> likelihood of this being useful / relevant increases"

**4. Loop / recurrence count.**
> "recall based on heuristic criteria, like if 3 loops of this toolcall occured this lesson might be
> applicable"

Not "this happened" but "this has now happened N times." Prior art: the innermost-loop-iteration
counter is valuable enough in branch prediction that a dedicated loop predictor was later dropped
because IMLI subsumed it.

**5. Execution stage.**
> "by execution"

Where in a piece of work this is — proposal, mid-flight, verification, wrap.

### Reached by what is true right now

**6. Environmental variables.**
> "recall from environmental variables"

**7. Signals — tendencies, patterns, conditions.**
> "We can also mint sensors and logics for recall that allow certain tendencies or patterns or
> conditions to flag certain kinds of recall ... This way we aren't only tied to toolcall. It
> expands our domain awareness"

MINTED, like verbs. A sensor needs a contract: what it reads, how fresh, and what it confesses it
cannot see.

**8. Threat level.**
> "on threat level"

What it costs to be wrong here. Distinct from confidence: this is the stake, not the certainty.

**9. Temperature.**
> "on temperature"

Hot (must arrive unasked, mid-task) versus cold (waits to be asked). Navi's rule sharpens this: a
category earns its place only if it routes to a different *moment*.

### Reached by time

**10. Time-based matching.**
> "we can have time based matching"

**11. Frequency.**
> "frequency"

How often, as distinct from how recently and from how many times in a row.

### Reached by meaning

**12. Similarity.**
> "on similarity"

The only dimension currently implemented, and the flattest one.

**13. Thematic.**
> "thematic recall"

**14. Pattern / heuristic.**
> "pattern or heuristic recall"

### Reached by who and under what rules

**15. Personality.**
> "by personality"

Which seat is acting. Carries the subject law: a flag about seat Y is labelled as about seat Y.

**16. Operating framework.**
> "by operating framework"

Under which rules this seat is currently working.

## The lifecycle that rides on all of them

> "each category has their own pool of lessons that get added or retired"

Per-category pools. Lessons join on evidence and retire on disuse or supersession. Retirement
ARCHIVES, never deletes — a retired capability that vanishes gets re-derived for months. Nothing
adds or retires itself; the adjudication register is reader-scoped.

## What the whole thing is for

> "This will enable us to basically have adaptive recall on the fly that changes depending on the
> task at hand, we have so many ways to cleverly tie this into our system design goals and wisdoms"

> "this granularity is what will enable incredible fidelity and percision, this maps back to the
> depth of the corpus being more specifically mapped out and tied into, even if its just this axis,
> thats the power of this"

### The original proposal, superseded, retained for its evidence

Every dimension above is a property of the **situation**. None of them would have connected Daniil's
"multifaceted lens" idea to his "recall on many dimensions" idea — those two share no vocabulary, no
domain, and no moment. They share only a SHAPE: many named axes, each with bounded reach and its own
confidence.

**A STRUCTURAL dimension — reach by the shape of a thing rather than its subject — is missing from
the set, and its absence is what let two isomorphic ideas sit unconnected in one house while the
seat holding both failed to see it.**

## Honest state

Sixteen dimensions named. ONE implemented (similarity). The dimension count is a design, not a
capability, and a design that has been true for a while and unbuilt is not yet an advantage over a
simpler thing that runs. What changes that is the shelf: a dimension can now be fielded as a
challenger against the incumbent on a bounded sample, and kept or dropped on evidence.

## AUTHORSHIP RULE FOR ANYTHING ADDED BELOW THIS LINE

Appended 2026-08-27 after Kimi named the seam: **the DIMENSIONS are Daniil's; the REGISTER — the
typing, grouping and ordering they are collapsed onto — is the fleet's.** Conflating the two is how
a re-typing becomes a re-authoring.

Navi's round-2 re-typing was legitimate precisely because it **re-typed Daniil's objects without
re-deciding them**: it found a type error in how the set was organised and changed nothing about
what any dimension *is*.

So, binding on every future edit:

  1. A dimension ADDED by a seat is marked `[fleet-proposed, <seat>]` and stays marked. It does not
     silently join the numbered set as though the operator had named it.
  2. RE-TYPING, GROUPING and ORDERING are the fleet's to change freely — that is the register, not
     the design.
  3. CHANGING WHAT A DIMENSION IS, merging two of his into one, or dropping one, is a decision for
     Daniil, not a seat. Propose it in the margin; do not perform it in the list.
  4. `[fleet-proposed]` entries carry the same evidence burden as anything else and can be killed by
     any seat — #17 STRUCTURAL was mine, proposed and killed inside one round.

The register grows. The design is asked for.
