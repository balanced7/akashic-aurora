## The law (one sentence)

A ceremony earns its place iff it carries the triple: an EVENT TRIGGER (a mechanical transition
fires it — never "regularly"), a CATCH-RECEIPT (proof it caught/changed something, not proof it
ran), and a KILL RULE that does not route through another unfired ceremony.

The doctrine already states the cure (ORG Part 7: "a contract is inert until projected into organs
that fire at the moment it applies"). The censuses are the inventory of remaining inertness.

## Long-horizon answer (for the operator's question, distilled)

Long-horizon work fails at seams — session death, handoffs, silence, rot. Storage is solved here
(atoms/notes/lessons); what rots is OBLIGATIONS (asks without expectations) and CALIBRATION
(bets without scoring). Moves 2-3 are the direct answer: every obligation carries an expectation
that pages, every receipt carries a TTL that pages, and the pages land at boot where the next
seat — ANY next seat — inherits them. Continuity through receipts, not through memory.
