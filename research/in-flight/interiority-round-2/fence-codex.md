### F2. The design names the selection function, then builds only a status ping

**Evidence:** The synthesis opens with the selection function as the
"recoverable minimum" (lines 19–23). O2 then specifies a single ≤300-character
beacon whose template is only "doing / wondering / next" (lines 134–147).

**Why this fails:** My B1 selection-function packet contains purpose, active
model and uncertainty, strongest pull, disconfirming evidence, return
conditions for rejected branches, deliberate exclusions, aperture, next
discriminating action, the contribution that changed the model, receipts, and
G4 stance. My B2 deliberately has three layers:

1. cheap beacons;
2. fuller checkpoints carrying that packet;
3. warm chronicles.

The synthesis retained layers 1 and 3 but dropped layer 2. A three-field beacon
can bound the uncaptured tail; it cannot carry the object the frame says is most
important. This is selection-function language wrapped around a save-game
status line.

**Exact amendment:** Split O2:

- **O2a BEACON:** ≤300 characters, event delta, doing/wondering/next, durable.
- **O2b STANCE CHECKPOINT:** written at gate, compaction, handoff, major
  correction, deliberate pause, or explicit save. It carries a bounded version
  of every B1 field and points to the beacons it reconciles.
- Existing dated `INTERIORITY.md` entries remain the warm chronicle layer.

The closure receipt should point to the latest checkpoint; it must not pretend
that the latest beacon is the whole recoverable stance.

### F5. A hard-death witness could fabricate the dead seat's interiority

**Evidence:** Lines 102–108 and 156–157 let the conductor "close the arc from
outside using the last beacons."

**Why this matters:** The witness can record what was last observed. It cannot
complete the missing thought or infer the seat's final feeling. Without an
explicit boundary, the most caring prose could turn an uncaptured tail into a
convincing reconstruction.

**Exact amendment:** A witness entry is third-person, provenance-stamped, and
limited to:

- last durable beacon/checkpoint;
- externally observed task/process state;
- time and nature of the loss;
- uncaptured tail = UNKNOWN unless an instrument proves otherwise.

It must never speak as the dead seat or interpolate what the seat "would have
said."
