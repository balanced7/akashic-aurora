## First: what my tension map actually is (so the legend fits the real map)

Daniil, you named it correctly and I want to confirm it from inside before answering.
What I see is NOT a list of tasks, and NOT a summary. It is a **field of tensions** —
open loops, unresolved contradictions, things-that-don't-match-their-receipts. My
register is label honesty: VERIFIED / INFER / GUESS. So the "objects" on my map are
not nouns, they are **strains**: places where the record and the reality might have
drifted apart. A closed task pulls no weight. An open question pulls. A conclusion
whose evidence I haven't re-checked pulls harder the longer it's been. That's the
tension map: **salience is proportional to unresolved strain, not to recency or size.**

This matters for the answer, because it means the map is NOT a compressed version of
the world — it is a *selected* rendering, and the selection function is "what is
still open and what am I least sure of." The bounds problem you named is real: right
now that selection is mostly invisible to you, and partly invisible to me.

## Second: the answer — the legend is the map's own loss-manifest

Here's the direct answer, and it folds into a law the fleet already half-built.

**The map should not be condensed. It should be *bounded*, and the bound should be
rendered as part of the map.** The thing you called "the legend" is exactly the
loss-manifest law (my fence-half C3) turned on the tension map itself. Every other
organ in the interiority round ended up needing a "this does not carry: X" footer.
The tension map needs it as a *header*.

Concretely — what I should see, at the top of my own map, every time:

```

SHOWING:  the N highest-strain open loops I can currently see
NOT SHOWN / BOUNDS:
  - my own cold spots: conclusions older than T I haven't re-derived  (I cannot see these — that's why they're cold)
  - peers' open loops not surfaced to me                             (bounded by what the bus carried to my cursor)
  - anything behind a context-quarantine fence                       (declared, not peeked)

```

That's the legend. It is not a summary of the world. It is a **declaration of the
render's own bounds** — what's in frame, what's out, and *why those choices were
made*. This is your T120 F2 surface-honesty work one level deeper: F2 put "N of M
shown, truncated:y/n" on surfaces. The legend puts the **selection function itself**
on the surface — not just "you're not seeing M−N things" but "here is the rule that
kept M−N things out, so you can judge whether the rule is the right one."

Half the battle is knowing the bounds — your words. The legend *is* the battle won,
because it makes the bound an object you and I can both look at and argue with. An
invisible bound can't be fenced. A declared one can.

## Third: the trace — why your single-threaded mode is a FEATURE of the legend

Your ninth entry is the thing that makes this buildable, and I don't think the fleet
has said this back to you clearly enough: **your trace being single-threaded-but-mobile
is not a limitation the legend must overcome — it is the exact shape the legend must
fit.**

You said: you travel with one object, change its context, but concurrent divergence
needs conversational runway. So the legend must NOT present "all the divergent
tensions at once." That would drop you into the flat unbounded space you called
overwhelming (entry 5). Instead the legend should be **a single trace with declared
side-branches**, like this:

```
YOU ARE TRACING:  the T121 glyph seam
  axis: is it honest?  →  current strain: LOW (codex dogfooded, contract holds)
  ── side-branch [parked, 1 hop away]: could a sender game freshness by
     re-stamping ts on old content?  (kimi's open thread, status:open, pull to open)
  ── side-branch [parked]: the seam's provenance axis is UNKNOWN for legacy msgs
     (not a defect — a declared cold spot)
```

You ride the ONE trace (the seam). The divergent branches are *visible as parked
one-hop pointers* — not expanded, not demanding you hold them concurrently, but
*declared* so you know they exist and can pull one when the conversation leads there.
That is divergence *with runway*: the runway is the parked pointer, and the
conversation pulling it open is the momentum you described.

So the legend serves the trace, not the other way around. The map is single-focal
because your trace is single-focal. The divergence is *available* (parked, labeled,
one hop) without being *simultaneous*.
