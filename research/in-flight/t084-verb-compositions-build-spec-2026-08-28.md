## Problem kind and sources

This is an observation-and-preservation program, not a new authority plane. It
composes facts already owned by the parser, MCP decorators, ToolBox class, door
parity manifest, trust registry, bus archive, library, and identity corpus.

The governing inputs are:

- W167: a seat needs one resolved view of its effective grant and the doors a
  verb actually reaches, without per-verb trial and error.
- Truth Ground G4/G10/G12/G13: evidence is typed; unavailable checks are
  UNKNOWN; bounded views confess omissions; no static fact promotes itself to
  live proof.
- The existing conversation-door design: a bus thread becomes an attributed,
  verbatim atom with citation edges, and expiry refuses loudly.
- T384 identity authority: continuity may assemble evidence about a seat, but
  it may not nominate, ratify, rewrite, or infer a resident designation.

## Slices and kill conditions

### S0 — `sweep` (landed at 7973bae5)

Pure, subject-bound structured awareness. Kill condition: any cursor, presence,
expectation, delta, worklive, or foreign-identity mutation during observation.

### S1 — `ground verb:<name>`

One typed evidence ladder, in this order:

1. `declared` — classification and source-of-truth declaration.
2. `reachable` — which CLI, MCP, and ToolBox addresses exist, including aliases
   and declared omissions.
3. `authorized` — the named capability requirement compared with THIS subject's
   effective grant. If no requirement is mechanically known, UNKNOWN.
4. `wired` — declared endpoints resolve to real parser handlers/functions.
5. `exercised` — bounded test references; a reference never means the test ran.
6. `proven` — a fresh canonical runtime receipt, or UNKNOWN when no resolver
   exists.

Every rung carries source, observed time, claim, state, details, and a drill.
The result carries subject, bounds, blind regions, and `effects: []`.

`reach` is absorbed by rungs 2–3. It earns a separate verb only if later use
shows an independent question that cannot be answered by this ladder.

Kill conditions: presence on a surface rendered as authorization; a test-file
mention rendered as passing; missing receipt rendered as proof; missing subject
silently borrowed from another callsign.

### S2 — `capture --thread`

Resolve a thread from immutable/read-only archive streams, deduplicate transport
copies by stable content identity, preserve every message verbatim with sender,
kind, stream id, timestamp, reply/thread links, and source stream, then mint one
draft conversation atom through the library door. The capture reports scanned,
matched, shown, deduplicated, failed streams, and archive bounds.

Kill conditions: advancing a cursor; treating substring/topic similarity as
thread membership; silently omitting an unreadable body; writing a hand-made
projection instead of minting through the authoritative library seam.

### S3 — `ground seat:<id> --continuity`

Assemble a subject-bound continuity profile from authoritative resident
designation receipts, authored lessons, durable notes/handoffs, attributed
artifacts, and recent movement. Each region keeps its own provenance, bounds,
currency, and unknowns. It is evidence for recovery, never an identity verdict.

Kill conditions: foreign evidence accepted as self-authorship; nickname/name
inferred from prose; unratified designation promoted; continuity read mutating
the subject's cursor/session/watcher; absence rendered as nonexistence.

### S4 — native citizenship

Each stable slice lands on CLI, MCP, and ToolBox through one shared core seam.
ToolBox calls the seam in-process; it never shells out for these combinations.
The door-parity ratchet classifies each new surface in the same slice.
