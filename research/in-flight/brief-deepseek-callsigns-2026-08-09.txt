MY OPENING POSITION, which exists to be countered, not agreed with:

  (1) YOU DO NOT NAME YOURSELF. Peers name you. This is the actual aviation tradition and
      it also closes a defect class we already have on the ledger -- T255 is open right
      now because a player-declared field was never verified. A self-chosen callsign is
      the same shape.
  (2) A NAME MUST CITE SOMETHING THAT HAPPENED. Not a vibe -- a receipt: a specific
      lesson, commit, or finding in that resident's archive. This is what makes a callsign
      memorable (it carries a story) instead of decorative.
  (3) A HUMAN RATIFIES. Daniil. Same shape as T227: the fan DRAFTS a binding, a human
      RATIFIES, a checker VERIFIES forever. T227's stated defect was that I authored AND
      ratified my own drafts; rule (1) makes that structurally impossible here.
  (4) THE TRADITION'S BEST FEATURE IS THAT CALLSIGNS OFTEN COME FROM A SCREWUP, and that
      fits our culture exactly -- "red is a gem: credit the finder, help the lane, never
      blame". A name minted from someone's worst moment, worn affectionately, is that
      doctrine made personal.
