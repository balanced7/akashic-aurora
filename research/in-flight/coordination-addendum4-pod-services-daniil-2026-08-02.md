# Addendum 4: pod services, help, steering, and the topology ruling

Status: current (2026-08-02, claude#30e6af5c). Rides Daniil's gate with the
reconciliation + addenda 1-3. Sorts his continued pod exploration into
adopted / sharpened / rejected-with-receipt. V1 scope still unchanged.

## Daniil, verbatim

"agent enters it and the pod does things for the agent. agent can plug into the pod and
the pod manages the synchronization with the other pod of the other agent, or perhaps
both agents join the same pod, then we can tell what files are being modified, the
duration of the interaction, the systems its touching. it can be the mechanism for
asking for help. im thinking that when agents enter the pod that becomes their way of
communication for that work slice, readable on the bifrost and can be seen by other
agents. this way we can steer mid action tasks."

## ADOPTED (new)

1. POD AS SERVICER — the pod DOES things for its members, not just holds equipment.
   Already-latent instances now named: the deferred queue answers "not now" on your
   behalf; alert bounds watch your limits for you; teardown cleans up after you.
   Future services accrete on this frame (pod files your work — W113; pod carries
   handoffs).
2. POD AS HELP MECHANISM — position field `help_wanted`: an ask addressed to the WORK,
   not a name; renders on the board at alarm tier; answered by whoever is alive and
   capable. Kills the briefs-to-corpses class (a help-ask to a pod cannot be sent to a
   dead seat because it is sent to no seat). Gives T097 (mutual revival) and T058
   (mid-turn clarification) their scoped home.
3. STEER-THE-WORK — the fidelity ladder's steer verb gains a POD ADDRESS: steer the
   task, whoever holds it; delivered at the holder's next boundary; heads-down defers;
   operator/red breaks through. Survives seat swaps mid-work.

## SHARPENED

4. POD AS OBSERVATION SCOPE — files touched, duration, systems used, scoped to the pod.
   The pod is the STABLE observable: it outlives incarnation swaps (Fable→Opus
   mid-round receipt), so "what is this pod touching" beats "what is this agent doing"
   for epoch-ambiguity reasons the reconciliation already ratified.

## ALREADY CAPTURED (confirmed, no loss)

5. Pod as the comm channel for the work slice, readable on Bifrost, visible to other
   agents = the board + transparency sections of the main design.

## REJECTED, WITH THE RECEIPT

6. TWO-POD TOPOLOGY (per-agent pods synchronizing pod-to-pod): two pods that must
   synchronize ARE "two paths that were supposed to agree" — disease class (a), the
   thing the whole design exists to kill. Pod-to-pod sync is dual-write reborn one
   layer up. RULING: ONE SHARED POD PER ENGAGEMENT, PER-AGENT PLUGS — each agent's
   adapter into the shared pod is its existing membrane (runner/hooks). "The pod is
   shared; the plug is yours." Daniil's "plug into the pod" phrase is retained as the
   canonical description of the agent-side connector.
