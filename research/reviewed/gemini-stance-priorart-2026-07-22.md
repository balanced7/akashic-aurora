## The verdict (verbatim)

**(a) Top 3 transferable mechanisms:**
- **Context Flushing at Phase Gates:** purge outdated execution traces and inject the stance
  card directly into working memory to eliminate context pollution.
- **Schema-Bound Tool Routing:** map stance attributes directly to constrained tool
  permissions for the incoming phase.
- **Pre-Execution Compliance Signalling:** require explicit verification that planned actions
  align with the stance card before tool invocation.

**(b) Mechanism NOT to copy:**
- **Raw History Accumulation:** appending stance cards onto legacy conversation histories
  causes attention dilution, instruction conflict, and stance degradation.
