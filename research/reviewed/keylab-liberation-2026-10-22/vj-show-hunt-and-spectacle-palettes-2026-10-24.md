## 1. How I measured

- **Inputs.** ref-3 … ref-11 (2000 px wide WebP). ref-3 and ref-4 are byte-identical to the Berlin lane's berlin-ref-1 and berlin-ref-2, so the two lanes' numbers cross-check.
- **Masking.** Each frame is cropped to its video area. The player bar, title, Q watermark, corner frame and the loading spinner in ref-3 are masked out.
- **The frames are SDR screenshots.** Bright light clips at code 255. Every highlight and headroom figure is therefore a lower bound, and the HDR levels below are design choices, not measurements.
- **What each frame gets:**
  - **Air:** the darker half of the sky box. Where the sky is true black, I use the soft-lit haze instead.
  - **Near-black:** max channel ≤ 16. **True black:** max channel ≤ 3.
  - **Highlights:** any channel ≥ 245.
  - **Hottest pixels:** the top 0.5% by linear luminance. I record where they sit and whether they form points, streaks or blobs.
  - **Hue families:** weighted by how much light each carries (saturation × value²).
  - **Saturation:** by brightness band, and in soft smoke versus sharp beams and sparks.
  - **Named regions** picked by eye.
  - **Element details:** spark density and size, streak length, sheet texture, the ring's falloff, CO2 column widths, the stage glow by height, and the crowd.

### What the numbers say about colour

1. **The air is a saturated canvas, not grey.** The Berlin dusk air is a mid-dark blue: teal-blue at OKLCh hue 240 in ref-3, indigo at 265–268 in ref-4 to ref-6. Lit smoke is the most saturated thing in the laser frames (HSV S 0.78–0.96). It stays above 0.74 up to V 0.75 and only whitens above V 0.9. Pyro smoke is less saturated (S 0.58) because warm and cool sources mix in it.
2. **Green is an accent by weight.** Green carries only 4–5% of the light in ref-3 and ref-4, and 21% in ref-5, yet reads strongly. Two families plus white is plenty.
3. **Light changes hue as it whitens, and the direction depends on the colour.** Measured from the dark end of each ladder to the core:

   | Colour | Ladder (OKLCh L / C / hue) | Hue shift |
   |---|---|---|
   | Blue sheet (ref-3) | air .31/.07/240 → halo .61/.12/234 → bright .84/.11/222 → core .97/.05/196 | −44° (toward cyan) |
   | Ring (ref-11) | glow .49/.22/263 → ring .87/.10/208 | −55° |
   | Violet (ref-10) | beam .58/.24/313 → CO2 .81/.14/329 | +16° (toward pink) |
   | Gold (ref-7) | halo .57/.10/54 → tail .78/.10/71 → core .97/.03/79 | +25° (toward yellow) |
   | Green (ref-5) | halo .68/.18/151 → core .94/.10/156 | +5° |

   `glowHotCore` (`E:/AI-Setup/arsenal/web/glsl/light/glow.glsl`) whitens toward equal channels, so it cannot make this shift. I propose a new helper rather than changing the shared one (see §3).
4. **Laser cores stay tinted; pyro cores go white.** Among the hottest pixels, only 2–24% are white (S < 0.15) in the laser frames, against 53–81% in the pyro frames. In HDR, give laser cores the palette's tinted W and pyro sparks true white-hot cores.
5. **Smoke takes the colour of the light inside it.**
   - Dense CO2 hit by violet beams reads 15–25° pinker and lighter than the beam (CO2 hue 329–337 against the beam's 313).
   - Green beams in blue haze read teal (#0b8888, hue 195).
   - Gold comets in violet smoke keep peach-white heads (#fed1bf), but their halos turn violet (#733c77).
6. **The "blue-white" glitter is barely blue.** The cool sparks measure #9c9dbe (chroma 0.048). They read blue because they sit next to warm gold sparks: 73% cool in the blue-white zone, 75% warm in the gold zone. Low-chroma glitter paired with gold is enough.
7. **Headroom.** Even clipped, the hottest 0.5% sits 3.9–4.7 stops above the air in the Berlin frames, 5.7–7.8 in the finale, and 7.7 at the ring.

## 3. Spectacle vocabulary: how each looks, how to draw it cheaply

All recipes are for the WebGL2 fragment-shader rig. They write scene-linear light (≥ 0, finite, no clamp, 1.0 = SDR white) and leave the tone curve to `displayOut` (`post/display-out.glsl`) or the HDR output path. Air and smoke stay at their measured low levels; the HDR headroom goes only to small areas. Light is always added, never mixed. Pixel figures are at 2000 px wide.

1. **Sheet beams.**
   - *Look.* ref-3's cyan fan runs from halo #1a8fc2 through bright #70dcff to core #ccffff. Its texture is an even ~10% ripple at every scale (dense beams plus mist). ref-5's green sheet is blotchy instead: texture is 5% at fine scale, 13% at mid scale, 26% at coarse scale, with clumps about 2–3% of frame height. Its core stays green (#2fe57e, S 0.8).
   - *Render.*
     - Angle mask from the origin: `domToPolar`, with `aaFill` on the angular edges.
     - Density: 2–3 octave `noiseValue3` or `fbmGrad2` at a 2–3% scale, drifting in units per second.
     - Light = colour × level × (0.55 + 0.45·density) × distance fade × `phaseHG` (g 0.6). Add striation 0.85 + 0.15·cos(N·θ), with N ≥ 40.
     - Brighter leading and trailing edge lines.
     - Body 0.3–0.6, edges 1.5–3. Cost O(1) per fan.
   - *Safety.* Sheets are large-area, so they are scene light only: fades of at least 150 ms, never gated. Lasers v2 forbids sheet forms in gated lasers (spec §4.1 rule 4), so they must not go through the laser gates.
2. **Thin-beam lattices.**
   - *Look.* ref-4: pale cyan-white beams (#8ec9da, chroma 0.065) with green as 4% of the light. The beams break into short dashes: median 17 px long, 2.4 px wide (ref-6 beams are 1.9 px wide). Crossings form bright knots (37% of the hot area is blobs).
   - *Render.*
     - Nearest beam of each fan in polar repeat (`domRepeat2` on angle), core `aaWire` 1.5–2.5 px, halo `glowLorentz` about 6 px.
     - Dash mask: `smoothstep` of `noiseValue2` at 2–4% scale, so beams show only where they cross haze clumps.
     - Crossings add up to white on their own.
     - Fan geometry (far-beam spacing 4–9°) is in the Berlin lane's `measure_fans`.
3. **Searchlight beams.**
   - *Look.* ref-6: near-horizontal cones from the tower tops. Core #b2bfcf (S 0.15), halo #407383.
   - *Render.* A Gaussian angular cone 1.5–3° wide, with brightness along it × `phaseHG(dot(beam, view), 0.7)`, a slow fbm ripple, and a source spot from `glowHotCore` at 5×. Body 0.4–0.8.
4. **Lit smoke and CO2 jets.**
   - *Look.* ref-10 CO2:
     - Columns are 24–34 px wide (FWHM) at the base, 47–78 px mid-column and 62–116 px at the top (about a 4–5° half-angle).
     - They are brightest at mid-height, where luma reaches 0.63–0.71.
     - Hue rotates +15–25° toward pink compared with the violet beam that lights them, and chroma stays high (0.14–0.20).
   - *Render.*
     - Cone mask from the nozzle, density from `warpFbm2` moving upward in units per second, and an envelope from `envRamp` (the v1 spec: 80 ms attack, 1.2 s fade).
     - Light = Σ(emitter colour × its falloff) × density, with at most 8 nearest emitters at quarter strength (as in v1).
     - Then `hueRotateOklch` by +20°·density (capped) and a lift in L.
     - If marched, use half resolution with `volStepScatter` and `phaseHG`.
5. **Glitter bursts.**
   - *Look.* ref-7 and ref-8:
     - 37–85 sparks per 100×100 px, each 6–10 px² (about 3 px across), covering 3–5% of the glitter band.
     - Cores are near-white (S 0.13–0.21). The colour lives in low-chroma halos: cool #9c9dbe and warm #d2a485 in ref-7; rose #dfa9ac and lavender #9781af in ref-8.
   - *Render.*
     - Hash grid with cells at 1.5–2.5% of height. `hash33` gives each spark its jitter, birth time and twinkle phase.
     - Core from `glowHotCore`, about 1.5 px, with a 3–4 px exponential halo. Twinkle with a hashed per-spark flicker every 60–120 ms.
     - The shell grows as r = R(1 − e^(−t/τ)), then falls under gravity and dies within 1.2–1.8 s.
     - Check the 3×3 neighbour cells (9 evaluations), and only inside the band. Cores 6–8×.
6. **Willows, comets and rising gold streaks.**
   - *Look.* Willows (ref-7, ref-8): long streaks reach 25–30% of height (p90), 3–4 px wide. Cores #fff4e2 / #fffaed, amber halo #a3663b. Comets (ref-9): 15 tails with a median length of 16% of height, 5 px wide. Tail gold #de956f, peach head, violet halo inside the smoke.
   - *Render.*
     - Ballistic path p0 + v·t + ½·g·t², a pure function of launch time.
     - The trail is the last τ of the path in 4–6 segments, using `neonPathStep` and `neonLine`, fading from head to tail with a hashed crackle.
     - Willows: low speed, strong droop, 2.5–3 s life.
     - Heads 6×, tails 0.8–1.5. Only pixels inside a streak's bounding box test it.
7. **Sparkler gerbs and cold-spark fountains.**
   - *Look.* ref-7's stage zone is the densest: 85 sparks per 100×100 px, 6 px², core #e0c6be. The smoke they light reads #caa678.
   - *Render.*
     - Narrow cone (±8–15°), speed about 0.5 frame heights per second, 0.5–1 s life, with gravity.
     - K = 6–12 hashed particle slots, each placed analytically at t mod life.
     - Cold sparks: shorter (10–20% of height), smaller, warm white, almost no smoke.
8. **The glowing ring in haze.**
   - *Look.* ref-11:
     - The inner disc peaks at 32–40 px radius (luma 0.85) and the ring line at 64–72 px (0.53, about 6.5% of height).
     - The glow tail falls from 0.33 to 0.085 by 280 px (about 27% of height).
     - The ring is cyan-white #7ae7f7; the glow 60 px out is saturated blue #124fdd.
   - *Render.*
     - Ring: `sd2Circle` drawn with `aaStroke`.
     - Glow: `glowTwoGauss` with a wide tail, × (0.7 + 0.3·fbm) for the haze.
     - Colour runs from W at the line to A further out (`mixOklch`).
     - Ring line 2.5–3×, glow 0.15–0.3.
9. **The stage glowing through its own smoke.**
   - *Look.* ref-7, above the roof: luma 0.67 → 0.32 within about 60 px, then 0.19, 0.11, 0.07 down to the sky's 0.05. It roughly halves every 7% of frame height. Hue drifts from 73 through 49, 15 and 3 to the sky's blue: the glow reddens, then fades into the sky. ref-9's stage blows out to #b0d3f5 / #f7eff6.
   - *Render.* A silhouette at 0 with emissive edges, plus a smoke band of height-field fbm lit by stage colour × e^(−h/0.07H) × density, with `hueRotateOklch` toward red by up to 20° as it thins. Ember 0.5–0.8, and steady when held.
10. **Foreground silhouettes.**
    - *Look.* Crowd median #05080f–#0a0911 (Y 0.002–0.003), 44–53% near-black at dusk and 95% at night. Phone lights: 2.4–6.2 per 100×100 px at dusk, 14 at night, cool white; in ref-11 they pick up the show's violet (#9f5cfd).
    - *Render.* An fbm-bumped horizon at 80–88% of height filled with exactly 0, plus a hashed 1–2 px light field at 0.1–0.25 tinted by the palette air. This also gives the headline bar its near-occluder depth cue.
11. **Hot cores (shared by all of the above).**
    - The frames whiten toward W, not toward grey. Proposal: a new helper, written fresh and not a change to the shared module, `glowHotCoreTo(tint, i, coreTint)` = i · mix(tint, normalise(W)·peak, i²/(1+i²)).
    - Laser palettes use their tinted W; pyro palettes use white-hot.

## 4. Scene: Pyro Finale v2

**Aim.** Fix the judge's must-fix list for v1 (`vj-headline-prototypes-and-judge-2026-10-24.md:84-92`) and use what these frames teach.

**Composition** (positions in % of frame height from the top):
- **Sky:** void at exactly 0. The Gold & Glitter air #090d13 is allowed only above the smoke band.
- **Smoke band** at 35–70%: static fbm, lit only by the pyro and the stage.
- **Stage:** a central silhouette, base at 82% and top at about 45%. Its emissive panels use the palette ground, glowing through smoke as in vocabulary item 9.
- **Crowd:** the bottom 13% at 0, with phone lights.
- **Glitter curtain:** full width at 30–55%, made of dense shells (vocabulary item 5) instead of v1's asterisks.
- **Comets:** mirrored fans of 8–10 comets each from emitters behind the stage, spread over 60–100°. Trails 16% of height.
- **Willows** in the outer thirds.
- **Lasers:** in the Pink Smoke palette, low green rakes at 3–15° stay above the crowd line, under a rose smoke ceiling.
- **CO2:** six jets, coloured by whatever light hits them; white only when the flash gate grants a white-out.

**Motion, locked to beat and bar.**
- **Clock.** Beat-in-bar and bar-in-phrase come from the strip. This fixes v1's off-by-one and needs the look-v2 lane's beat-in-phrase field.
- **Bar 1 (the one).** A comet fan volley sweeps one side, with launches staggered by 1/16 beat. Sides alternate each bar; both sides fire on a phrase's first bar.
- **Beat 3.** Glitter shells burst, expanding with τ = 0.08 beat, then crackle on the 8ths and 16ths.
- **Kicks.** Stage gerbs pulse for 250 ms and the ember breathes +15%. That is a breath, not a flash.
- **Off-beat.** The smoke backlight inhales +10–15%.
- **Phrase line.** A willow pair launches and falls through the next 1–2 bars.
- **Build.** Density doubles every 2 bars and colour drains to W in the last bar. The last beat is black.
- **Drop.** A full mirrored volley, and the glitter curtain lights from the centre outward over one beat (never in a single frame). The CO2 white-out is requested through the gate.

**Palettes by section.**

| Section | Palette |
|---|---|
| Build | Gold Willow |
| Drop and climax | Gold & Glitter |
| Drop 2 | Lasers Under Pink Smoke, or Magenta CO2 |
| Euphoric | Comets in Violet Smoke |
| Outro | Gold Willow, then cut to black |

Each section has at most two colour families plus white.

**HDR.**
- Spark cores 6–8×, comet heads 6×, willow cores 2–3×, ember 0.5–0.8, smoke ≤ 0.25, air ≤ 0.004.
- Area above 1.0: at most 1.5% of the frame in the groove and 4–6% on the drop. That matches the headline bar's white-hot budget, and it keeps big areas dim so the OLED's large-area limits are never hit.

**Safety.**
- No full-frame step changes.
- Twinkle phases are hashed per spark and at most 15% of sparks peak in any frame. Spark coverage is ≤ 8% of any one-third window, measured densities give 3–5%.
- The white-out only through the flash gate, at most once per 8-bar phrase.
- The held ember is steady, with red area change under 10% per 333 ms.
- Lasers obey the v2 caps.

**Cost.** Target ≤ 2.5k operations per pixel on average, against v1's 5–6.5k. These are estimates; it needs a real GPU timing with Daniel's OK.
- Glitter only inside its band (9 cells).
- Comets tested only in their bounding boxes, with 4–6 segments each.
- At most 6 live bursts per side.
- Smoke as 3-octave value noise in the band only, or at half resolution.
- Light from a uniform list of the 8 nearest emitters.

## 5. Scene: Kinetic Array

**From Daniel's words:** a giant indoor set with a ring, and elements over the crowd that rise and fall in waves, carrying lasers, sparklers and lights.

**Composition.**
- **Indoors:** void ceiling. Air is lit only where beams and cones pass.
- **Hero ring:** centred at 42% from the top, diameter about 60% of frame height. Built per vocabulary item 8, with the glow reaching about 3 ring radii.
- **The array:** 7 rows × 13 pods in perspective, receding from big near rows toward the base of the ring. Each pod is a rounded bar with an LED face, hung on two thin cables (the cables show its height). Each carries a laser head, a cold-spark gerb underneath and a down spot cone. Far rows lose contrast through `fogExp`.
- **Crowd:** silhouette across the bottom 15%.

**Motion.** Height = h0 + A·W(mode, row, column, bar phase, beat phase). It is computed purely from the clock, with no state, so it cannot drift off the beat. Modes change only on bar lines:

| Mode | What moves | Rate | Used in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Row wave | crest travels from the crowd toward the ring | 1 wave per bar | build, groove |
| Ripple | rings out from the hero ring (`patCellDelay`) | 1 per kick | drop |
| Checker | alternate pods up and down, eased over 0.35 beat | each beat (every 2 beats above 170 BPM) | drop, raw sections |
| Diagonal sweep | diagonal wave | per 2 bars | break |
| Breath | all pods rise together, then drop on the one with a small spring | over 4–8 bars | build into drop |
| Formations | arch or V shape held for a bar, morphing on the line | per bar | finale |

**Lights on the pods.**
- **LED faces:** level = 0.3 + 0.7 × closeness to the crest, so light travels with the wave. Crest cores go toward W.
- **Lasers:** use the v2 "wave" order, firing as the crest passes. Only one row or diagonal is lit at once, and the v2 caps (≤ 4 heads above 3 pulses a second) hold. Pod lasers aim up and out toward the ring. Downward light is the spot cones, because beams dim below the audience line.
- **Sparkler waterfalls:** fire when pods reach the bottom of their travel on the drop, or on the phrase in the finale. At most a quarter of the pods at once, for 0.6–1 s.
- **Ring:** +8% spring on the kick, a segment chase stepping every 1/8, and a crown of B beams on the drop.

**Palettes by section.**

| Section | Palette |
|---|---|
| Intro and anti-climax | UV Ring |
| Build | Searchlights (pod spots) |
| Climax | Berlin Sheets or Dusk Lattice (a laser sea from the array) |
| Drop 2 | Magenta CO2 (CO2 from the pods) |
| Finale | Gold & Glitter (sparkler waterfalls) |

**HDR.** Ring line 2.5–3×, ring glow 0.15–0.3, pod faces 1–1.5×, laser cores 3–4×, spark cores 6–8×, spot cones 0.2–0.5, haze ≤ 0.05, void 0. Area above 1.0: ≤ 2% in the groove, ≤ 5% on the drop.

**Safety.**
- The waves stagger brightness across the array; all pods peak together only on a gated phrase drop.
- The pods' lit change is capped by the v2 area estimator (≤ 8% of the frame for fast gates).
- Checker moves are eased, not stepped.
- Sparks are small-area. No red is used.

**Cost.** Target ≈ 1–1.5k operations per pixel (estimate):
- Per row, project its band and find the nearest column (≤ 7 rounded-box SDF evaluations per pixel).
- Each row's lasers as parallel-fan nearest-beam math.
- Sparks as 3 hashed cells per row.
- Ring O(1) and one 2-octave haze noise.
