## Fix 1: Reverse-Bass Gate with the crown

**must_fix:**
- NEW REGRESSION FROM THE ROUND-3 'PLAYS ALONE' HAND-OVER: a new hit's first pulse now lands late whenever any light from the hit before it is still alive, even a 1-3 % tail that lights 0.00 % of a window. Mechanism (traced): `_spawn` puts the handed-over hits in `H.waitFor`. `_evalHit` only chokes them (`_choke(h)` via `_firstDue`) in the frame the new first pulse is due. The new hit then waits until they are `done`, which comes one frame later at the earliest, and later still if a lit pulse is serving its minimum on-time. So the frame on the beat goes out dark and the pulse lights in the next one.
