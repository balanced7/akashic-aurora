**Production facts**
- **Event and line-up:**
  - Saturday 22 November 2014, GelreDome, Arnhem. The theme was "The Source Code of Creation" and the MC was Ruffian.
  - Line-up: Audiofreq & Technoboy (warm-up), Headhunterz (stage-opening show), Atmozfears, Frontliner, Noisecontrollers (anthem show), Ran-D, Crypsis, then Endymion (show), The Viper and Partyraiser to close.
  - Sources: the Q-dance trailer description and the line-up text in the Anthem Show description; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qlimax
- **Attendance:** sources disagree. 27,000 (https://hardstylemag.com/qlimax-2014/), about 30,000 (a Dutch fan blog, https://mitchelldhendriks.wordpress.com/2014/11/29/qlimax-2014-a-source-code-of-creation-je-was-geweldig/) or 32,000 (https://www.festivalling.com/reports/qlimax-the-source-code-of-creation-report/). Hardstyle Mag also gives the wrong date ("29. November 2014").
- **Anthem:** Noisecontrollers (Bas Oskam), "The Source Code of Creation", released 2014-10-27 (catalogue Be Yourself / Q-dance). Sources: `olBwIm-SF1g`, `HVK8-OqVPDI`, https://www.frontview-magazine.be/en/news/qlimax-the-source-code-of-creation
- **Stage concept:** a figure's head (an "open-minded man waiting to activate his DNA"), modelled on Alex Grey's painting "Oversoul". A small glittering ball slowly descended behind the head, and the stage started hidden behind white curtains. Sources: https://hardnews.nl/en/is-this-the-inspiration-for-the-qlimax-stage/ and Hardstyle Mag.
  - Most sources put the reveal with the Headhunterz show: the festivalling report, and many fan uploads titled "Stage Opening and Headhunterz Intro".
  - One fan blog puts it at the anthem instead.
- **The ring:**
  - Four LED circles around the head that rotated (Hardstyle Mag; festivalling's "like a chopper").
  - The hardware was 500 m² of DMT Pixelscreen P25 SMD Pro LED mesh supplied by Highlite, hung in circles above the stage. The outer circle was almost 50 m across and hung 40 m above the floor. Sources: https://www.av-entertainment.nl/nieuws/500m2-dmt-pixelscreen-p25-op-qlimax (22 Jan 2015) and Highlite's archived news page.
  - Highlite also lists Showtec Sunstrip Active DMX and Matrix 5x5 blinders as products used. Its "lighting designer / rental" field names "Pro / Dis3bution Frank Verstraeten", but DMT's own Facebook post gives the same name as the photo credit, so I don't trust it as a design credit.
- **The array over the crowd:**
  - Twelve large "DNA" elements hung across the middle of the dome above the crowd, together forming one DNA strand. They were meant to "break the wall between audience and DJ", carrying the idea "we are of the same DNA". Sources: Hardstyle Mag and the fan blog.
  - The blog says they changed position and started moving at 22:00. The "move up and down… packed with lasers, fireworks and lights" line in Hardstyle Mag is the reviewer guessing before the show, not a confirmed fact.
  - In 2015 Q-dance replaced the DNA strands with squares on a similar setup (https://hardstylemag.com/qlimax-2015-equilibrium-review/).
- **Lasers, fire, haze and screens:** lights, lasers (mostly green, fanned through the haze) and fire. The LED content used 3D objects and particles that made the whole stage seem to move (Hardstyle Mag). Lighting ran from 3 desks and the laser show from a certified laser operator; about 350 L-Acoustics boxes, most of them flown (Wikipedia, which describes Qlimax in general, not 2014 specifically).
- **Rigging:** Eventions Rigging B.V. built the 2014 rig, according to its own time-lapse `rKN2g4u_RgY`.

**Not found despite looking:**
- Who built and drove the moving array, and which motion system it used. WIcreations (a Belgian motion-control firm) drove Q-dance's moving stages in 2008, 2018 and 2019 with its own control system, but no source links it to 2014.
- The lighting designer and the laser company for 2014.

# Source Array: the Qlimax 2014 light-show study and the landing spec (2026-10-25)

## For Daniel, in plain words

- **The ring is a dish of LED bands.** It is one closed outer band plus three open arcs that step inward like a spiral. Radial slats behind them make a sunburst, and black speaker stacks hang in front.
  - In your ring frame (4:18) the light does not spin; I measured under 1° per second. The swirl you remember is in the shape of the bands, and the light texture on them shimmers in place.
  - That moment sits in a break, with no kick.
- **The array over the crowd is one strand of 12 rungs** down the middle of the hall, not a grid.
  - Each rung carries a lit cube at both ends, a dot strip on each arm, a dot matrix in the middle and four moving heads underneath.
  - The rungs move slowly: they rise, sink, roll, zigzag and twist.
  - The beat lives in the light they carry, not in the machine.
- **The beams lock to the beat in a way I could measure:**
  - The lime rows fire hardest from the off-beat into the kick: 4.6 times brighter there than just after the kick.
  - Warm cones answer right after the kick, twice as bright there as at their weakest.
  - The cubes on the rungs flash white on the first beat of every bar, then fade back over two beats.
- **Your biggest drop is a dark one.**
  - The loudest drop of the Headhunterz show (7:00) is lit on only 23% of the picture, carried by thin strobing lime beams.
  - The build before it was flooded violet, at 76% lit.
  - The quiet sections drop to under 2% lit.
- **The new scene, Source Array, puts all of this on our rig.** It uses our own ring, our own rungs, a crowd silhouette and haze that takes its colour from the light. It comes with five new colour themes measured from the show.

## What I measured, and how

- **Inputs.**
  - Your three frames. I measured them on the matched source frames: ref-17 = BtDJuU7_6K4 at 258.15 s, ref-18 = 423.4 s, ref-19 = 488.683 s.
  - The capture's frames under `E:/AI-Setup/state/arsenal/refs/qlimax2014/frames/`, the key-look and timeline sheets, and the rig time-lapse.
- **Frame metrics.** I took them on the picture area only (letterbox removed per video), with Python (cv2, numpy):
  - true black (max channel ≤ 3);
  - near black (≤ 16);
  - luma ≤ 25;
  - p5/p50/p95 luma;
  - lit (luma > 64);
  - hot (any channel ≥ 250);
  - white (luma ≥ 235 and saturation < 0.25);
  - hue families of lit pixels with saturation > 0.25;
  - a haze classifier (low-gradient pixels at luma 8–140 in the upper 75%): median colour, OKLCh, level and share;
  - lit share by thirds, the bottom-20% median, and colour ladders by brightness band.
- **Sample size.** 31 look sections and 12 musical sections, about 3,200 frames in all.
- **Timing.**
  - I demuxed the soundtracks with `py -m arsenal.media audio --demux` and beat-tracked them with librosa.
  - I grabbed 11 spans at the native 23.976 fps, 480×270, with `py -m arsenal.media grab --out <scratch>`.
  - Then I averaged per-frame hue-family shares by eighth of a beat, excluding cuts.
  - I checked the beat phase against the audio itself. The kick's click and punch peak 1/16–2/16 of a beat before librosa's beat, so a peak at 7/8 is on the kick's attack.
- **Grades.** [H] = my own measurement of the footage or audio. [M] = trade press or fan sources (the find report). [L] = my inference or a starting value to tune.
- **Limits.**
  - The footage is SDR YouTube video from a camera in haze. Blacks are lifted (p5 0–30) and highlights clip: in ref-19, 14% of pixels clip the red channel.
  - True-black and white-hot numbers from the footage are therefore not LED-wall numbers. The spec sets wall targets separately.
  - At 24 fps, strobes faster than 12 Hz alias, and beat-phase precision is about ±1 frame (±0.1 beat).

## 1. The light show's grammar

### 1.1 The ring
- **Structure** [H frames; M press].
  - It is a shallow dish (cone) of LED mesh behind and over the stage. The side view (Partyraiser intro 1:20) shows its rim leaning toward the crowd.
  - On the dish are one closed outer band and three open arcs. Each arc steps inward and back, so the front view reads as a spiral or question mark.
  - The innermost arc is a horseshoe around the centre, open at the bottom.
  - Behind the bands, radial LED slats form a sunburst: about 40–48 slats (red reveal 1:51, Atmozfears 20:20).
  - Every band is cut into radial segments: roughly 100+ on the outer band and about 25 visible on the inner horseshoe.
  - Press: 500 m² of DMT P25 mesh, outer circle almost 50 m across [M].
- **Black hardware in front.** Four line-array speaker stacks and small moving-head pods hang in front of the ring and slice it. In every ring frame they are the only true black and the main depth cue.
- **How its content moves** [H]. There are seven behaviours:
  1. **Solid colour hold for a section.** Red reveal 1:49–2:10, violet 2:52–3:23, blue 4:00–4:33, cyan 6:12–6:32: one hue per 20–35 s run.
  2. **Shimmer in place in breaks.** This is ref-17, inside a no-kick break (audio low band 0.04–0.18, kick share 0 at 4:10–4:30).
     - Rotation measured by polar phase correlation with the shot's static structure removed: median −0.5°/s (IQR −0.8 to −0.2) over a 7.6 s shot.
     - The ring's brightness by beat phase stays within ±2%.
     - The festival report that the circles spun "like a chopper" stays unconfirmed in our footage.
  3. **Accent flip.** At 1:59.2 the whole ring goes white for about half a beat, black for about half a beat (3 frames; only its outline dots survive), then back to red.
  4. **Dotted outline only, for an anti-climax.** At 2:15–2:22: 97% of the frame at luma ≤ 25, 1.5% lit.
  5. **Flame-fringe outline.** Ran-D 35:18: thin red band edges with flickering flame tongues on near-black, plus a few thin lime laser lines.
  6. **Green dotted outline with green laser fans** (Atmozfears 39:20, Endymion 1:36).
  7. **Full floods in builds.** Violet at 5:00–5:18: 76% lit, white-hot 2–29% where the bands blow out on camera.
- **Colour ladder.** The bands whiten toward cyan [H, ref-17]:

  | Brightness | Colour | OKLCh |
  |---|---|---|
  | Dark | #0f1465 | L 0.26, C 0.14, h 270 |
  | Mid | #585cc3 | L 0.53, h 278 |
  | Bright | #8a9bef | L 0.71, h 274 |
  | Cores | #a7d5ff / #a2e5ff | h 224–246 |

  - That is −25° to −50° toward cyan, and 65% of the hottest pixels are azure or cyan.
  - Pink accents: #aa48a8 (h 328) on the cone and stage slats.

### 1.2 The kinetic array
- **Count and layout** [H].
  - 12 rungs, counted on the floor in the rig time-lapse (rKN2g4u_RgY 1:30), hung in one line on the hall's long axis over the crowd.
  - Press calls them twelve "DNA" elements forming one strand [M].
  - Seen from under the strand (ref-19), the rungs recede in tiers toward the ring.
- **One rung (2014 design)** [H, CDJmJ6EM0n4 0:40 and ref-19]:
  - a long box truss in two arms, with crossed-brace joints near the ends;
  - a diamond node at the centre holding a 5×5 dot matrix;
  - a frosted light cube at each end;
  - an LED dot strip along each arm, about 10–14 dots;
  - 2 moving heads under each arm, so 4 per rung and 48 on the strand.
  - The fan console replay's list (12 motion objects, 48 Sunstrips, 12 5×5 blinders, 24 "LED water tanks") fits 12 rungs with 4 strips, 1 matrix and 2 end cubes each [L cross-check].
- **Formations seen** [H strips at 1–2 fps]:
  - a level line;
  - lowered into a line of glowing "pearls", with the rungs end-on (HH 7:40–7:57);
  - every rung rolled the same way by about 40° (Anthem 5:13–5:35);
  - adjacent rungs rolled opposite ways into a zigzag (Anthem 5:51–6:11);
  - a travelling sine or twisted helix (Noisecontrollers 52:00, Anthem 6:25);
  - from below, the cubes form a V or funnel while every head converges on one white knot below (Anthem 5:29–5:31);
  - lowered close over the crowd carrying pyro (HH 8:31).
- **Speed.** Formations change over seconds, not beats [M: read from strips; the camera moves too much for a number]. The machine is slow; the light on it is fast.
- **How its light moves** [H]:
  - **Cubes as a bar marker.** In the Anthem at 5:13–5:35 the cubes snap to white once per bar: at beats #813, 817, 821, 825, 829 and 833, every 4 beats, 0.03–0.26 beat after the detected beat. They fade back to violet over about 2 beats.
  - **Dot strips and heads in the molten hold (ref-19 span).** Brightness by beat phase stays within ±1%, with long steady runs. The lime beams cross in X's under the tiers and sweep slowly. Dot cores are white-hot (0.6% of the frame) inside an orange field.
  - **Late drop (HH 9:03–9:25).** Cubes flash white and white beams blast. White light peaks ¼–⅜ beat after the kick, which is the reverse-bass hit; the beat profile runs from −10% to +12%.
- **Pyro on the rungs** [H]:
  - **Gerbs.** Fountains fire up and out from both cube ends of all 12 rungs for a whole drop: Anthem 6:25–6:59, about 21 bars at 150 BPM. The rungs stay in a zigzag or helix meanwhile.
  - **Flames.** Tall single jets come from the cubes on build hits (Anthem 5:45, 5:58).

**Rules distilled from this:**
1. One look per section, held 20–35 s, changing at section lines.
2. The loudest drop is the darkest. Thin strobing beams in a dim room follow a flooded build.
3. Push-pull: lime from the off-beat into the kick, warm right after the kick.
4. The bar's first beat is marked by the lanterns.
5. Breaks have no beat lock: the content shimmers and colour walks slowly.
6. Anti-climax means near-total dark: the ring outline only.
7. An accent is about half a beat of white, then about half a beat of black, then the look again.
8. The machine is slow; the light is fast.
9. Pyro marks sections, not beats: gerbs sustain a drop, flames land on build hits, fountains on one hit after a dip.
10. Haze carries the colour: saturated, and black at the edges.

## 2. The landing spec: `vj-source-array`, "Source Array"

**Answers to:**
- ref-17: the spiral ring in violet haze with pink accents;
- ref-18: the lime rows raking down, with warm cones;
- ref-19: the strand of rungs receding into the ring, molten, with lime crossings and white-hot dot strips;
- Anthem 5:13–6:59: rolls, zigzags, the floor knot and gerbs on every rung;
- HH 7:40–7:57: the lantern line and its hue walk;
- HH 2:15 and Ran-D 35:18: outline-only anti-climaxes;
- Ran-D and Anthem: stage laser fans and the canopy.

**Target.** A giant spiral ring of our own design burns in violet haze at the far end of a dark hall. Overhead, a strand of 12 rungs of our own design hangs over a crowd silhouette and recedes straight into the ring's eye. The rungs carry lanterns, dot strips, moving heads and gerbs. They rise, sink and fold in slow, beat-locked formations, while their light snaps on the bar and their beams strobe from the off-beat into the kick. Rows of heads rake lime into the crowd from the side trusses; warm cones answer from the stage. At rest it is a dotted red ring outline in the dark. At the drop it is the strand dropping toward the crowd, beams blooming out of a white knot, and gerbs pouring off every lantern.

### 2.1 Composition
Coordinates are centred units, frame height = 1, +y up, at 16:9 (frame half-width 0.889).

- **One-point perspective.** The vanishing point is the ring's eye at (0, 0.05), 45% from the top: the hero point in Lasers v2.
  - Camera: p = f·(X/Z, Y/Z) + (0, 0.05), with f = 1.2 (vertical field of view about 45°) and a shift lens, so verticals stay vertical.
- **Ring** on planes around Z = 40.
  - Outer band radius 0.40, so the ring is 0.80 of frame height and touches 5% from the top.
  - Band radii 0.40 / 0.31 / 0.225 / 0.14; radial widths 0.035 / 0.040 / 0.045 / 0.050.
  - Each inner band sits a little deeper (Z + 1.5 per band, the dish), so camera drift gives parallax.
- **Strand.** Rungs i = 0…11 at Z_i = 3·1.25^i (3 → 34.9), hung at Y = 1.0, half-length 2.4.
  - The nearest rung spans past the frame at y 0.45, as ref-19 does.
  - Rung 5 sits at y 0.18, ±0.31 wide; rung 11 at y 0.084, ±0.083 wide.
  - The strand pours into the ring's eye, and near rungs occlude the top of the ring (a depth cue).
- **Crowd.** A silhouette below y −0.34 (the bottom 16%), exactly 0, with an fbm-bumped top edge and hands. Phone dots 1–2 px at 0.1–0.25, tinted by the theme's air colour.
- **Occluders.** Four hanging line-array silhouettes at x ±0.20 and ±0.40, from the top of the frame to y −0.02, 0.035–0.045 wide, exactly 0, each with a dim two-head pod beneath. They are generic venue hardware.
- **Side head rows.** Two trusses along the hall walls (X ±5.5, Y 1.4, Z 4→26), 8 heads each, converging toward the vanishing point from the upper corners. Beams aim down and inward at 55–70° below horizontal, mirrored.
- **Stage line** at y −0.30:
  - 6 laser fan heads at x ±0.10, ±0.28, ±0.46 (scene light);
  - 2 warm cone heads at x ±0.62.
- **True black by design.** In the groove: the crowd band (16%), the speakers (about 5%) and the corners beyond the haze falloff give 25–40%, with exactly 0 there.

### 2.2 Geometry (2.5D, analytic, no ray march)
- **Planes.** Every rung and every band lives on its own Z-plane: one exact ray-to-plane hit, then a 2D signed distance in that plane.
  - All rung motion (height, roll, wing fold, sideways sway) is an in-plane transform, so it costs nothing extra.
  - No pitch toward the camera.
- **Ring bands.**
  - Outer band closed (360°). Arcs 2, 3 and 4 open, spanning 300°, 250° and 220°, with gaps at 4, 8 and 6 o'clock. The step reads as a spiral, and the inner horseshoe opens downward over the eye.
  - Radial segments: 96, 72, 48 and 24, so each segment's arc is ≥ 12 px at 1080p and gaps ≥ 2 px (no moiré).
  - Angular terms use integer multiples of the angle, so there is no seam at ±π.
  - Behind them, 40 radial slats on a back plane at Z 44, at 0.1–0.3.
  - The eye: a white-hot core of radius 0.02 at the hero point. No figure, no head.
- **Rung (our design).**
  - A slightly bowed twin rail: two thin parallel bars, 2.5 px or more at mid-depth.
  - A round node at the centre carrying a ring of 8 dots.
  - A rounded capsule lantern standing vertically at each end.
  - A dot strip of 10 dots along each rail half. Dots stay ≥ 1.5 px; beyond the depth where they would shrink below that, they cross-fade into a continuous line by coverage.
  - 4 heads hang under the rails (2 per side).
  - Strokes ≥ 1.5 px (2 px preferred), with analytic coverage on every edge.
- **Beams.**
  - 3D beams with analytic in-scatter, from the planned `light/beam.glsl`: closest approach of the view ray to the beam axis, weighted by `phaseHG` with g 0.6, so beams aimed at the camera glow more.
  - The heads on one rung share an aim, so their beams lie in one sheet plane. Use one plane hit plus nearest-beam repetition per rung. Do the same for each side row.
  - Every beam starts at a visible lens, and the root is the brightest point. This answers the Void Star judge's must-fix ("white-hot roots, no bright outlines").
  - No beam above 30% of its peak in the bottom 20% of the frame.
- **Sparks and flames.** Gerbs are a narrow cone (±10–12°) from each lantern tip, rising 0.6–1.0 rung half-lengths, 0.6–1 s life, with gravity, hashed slots, ≤ 1.5 px. Flames are a tapered column per lantern (the planned `draw2/pyro.glsl` and `light/fire.glsl`). Both are evaluated only inside each lantern's bounding box.

### 2.6 Motion vocabulary

**Stateless formation law.** Each formation is a pure function of the bar clock: formation(phrase k) = deck(section seed, k). The move from formation k−1 to k uses smootherstep over the first 2 bars of phrase k, each rung lagging the one before by ⅛ bar (near to far), so moves ripple toward the ring.

- Slow-machine limits: ≤ 45° per bar of rotation, ≤ 0.5 rung half-lengths per bar of height.
- One exception: the drop's ONE.
- With u = i/11 and amplitude a = k2:

| Formation | Law | Mirror-safe |
|---|---|---|
| F0 Level | h = 0 | yes |
| F1 Wave | h = 0.35·a·sin 2π(u − bar/2): one crest along the strand, travelling to the ring every 2 bars | yes |
| F2 Wings (ours) | arms fold ±25°·a about the node, alternating V and Λ by rung | yes |
| F3 Roll | every rung rolled 40°·a, direction alternating each phrase (Anthem 5:13) | k8 > 0 only |
| F4 Zigzag | ±30°·a by rung (Anthem 5:51) | k8 > 0 only |
| F5 Helix | roll = a·(30°·i + 45° per 4 bars) | k8 > 0 only |
| F6 Breath | rise through a build; on the drop's ONE, sink 0.2 half-lengths in one beat on a damped spring (≤ 15% overshoot), then settle over 2 bars | yes |

**Beat.**
- Lantern punch: +40% on a 6 Hz spring with damping 0.4.
- Ring scale: +3%.
- Click: dot strips, node dots and ring band edges go white-hot for one frame (≤ 3 px).
- Tail: dot glow +35% over 300 ms.
- Head aims step on the kick, eased over the first 45% of the beat (the Cathedral's grammar).
- Shutters follow the section's gate (below).

**Bar.**
- Lanterns snap to W on the ONE and decay to the theme colour over 2 beats (measured).
- In drops, the ring's segment chase advances one segment group per beat, one direction per section, modulation ≤ 30%, never reversing.

**Phrase.**
- The theme holds for at least 8 bars.
- Formation and pattern decks are dealt here.
- A returning section returns to its look.

### 2.7 What the kick, bass, build and drop do

- **Kick.** The click, punch and tail above.
  - Warm cones (pin colour) answer within ¼ beat after the kick (measured 2.0×).
  - The background never rises: the black floor (p5) moves by ≤ 2 codes.
- **Bass (reverse bass on the off-beat).**
  - Head-row shutters open from the off-beat and ride into the kick: Lasers v2 G5 push-pull, with lime as side B and warm cones as side A (measured 4.6×).
  - Haze inhales +10%, cut at the kick when the style is raw, released over 35 ms when euphoric.
  - In the second drop (Rose Fans), white fan blasts peak ¼–⅜ beat after the kick (measured).
- **Build.**
  - The strand rises (F6).
  - The ring floods the build colour, then drains toward W in the last bar.
  - The lantern snap doubles every 2 bars: bar, then half bar, then beat.
  - Heads converge on the floor knot (3.1), with G9 rolls on the rows within the area cap.
  - Pilot sparks appear at the lantern tips.
  - The last beat is black, except the eye's pilot (≤ 0.5% lit).
- **Drop (the ONE).**
  - A ring white flip of about half a beat, requested through FlashGate (it spends a slot; if refused, the ring steps to the drop palette).
  - The strand sinks on its spring, and heads bloom out of the knot.
  - Gerbs run on every lantern for 8 bars if k6 > 0; after that they are rationed to the first kick of each phrase or to pads.
- **Drop groove.**
  - F1 wave, lime rows push-pull, lantern bar snap, ring segment chase.
  - Formations F3–F5 only when k8 > 0.
- **Climax hold or half-time (ref-19).**
  - Molten Array: a steady field with no strobes.
  - Lime crossings under the rungs, sweeping one sweep per 2 bars.
  - Dot strips steady and white-hot.
- **Break.**
  - Source Ring: the ring shimmers in place (no rotation, no beat lock).
  - The strand reads as a lantern line.
  - Lantern hue walks ≥ 90° across 8–10 bars, monotonic, ≤ 20° per bar (measured blue → red).
  - Heads off, or a slow Phase Wave.
- **Anti-climax.**
  - Red Spiral: the ring as a flame-fringe or dotted outline, lantern pilots at 5%, 1–2 thin lime lines.
  - ≥ 70% true black.
- **Outro.** Cut to black on the last beat, and hold.
