## Method caveat, stated by the reviewer himself

He labels it a "First-pass GPT evaluation." It is a model scoring repositories against
a rubric, and in the ordinary case that means scoring what a repository DOCUMENTS about
itself. That is a real signal and a shallow one, and it cuts in our favour: this house
documents itself obsessively. Recorded here so nobody downstream reads the 14 as
measured rather than judged.
