# critique:shortest-path

_Verbatim agent return, workflow wf_fc6a68ac-233, 2026-09-27._

## Verified defects, ranked

**D1 — The design axiom is false as implemented. Taps do not break the circularity; measured.** The whole design rests on "the beat reference must come from the human." It doesn't. `beat.js:198` snaps every emitted beat 80% onto the nearest onset group (`phaseGain: 0.8`, `beat.js:74`; `inner = max(60, 0.18·p)` ≈ 114 ms at p=634), and `beat.js:487` hard-sets `a.next = g.t` on a gap. The tap path (`beat.js:405-437`) only *selects or seeds an agent's period*; phase still comes from the notes. Run against both artifacts on disk:

| | on-beat notes | **exactly 0.000 ms** | remaining mean |
|---|---|---|---|
| `lens6full` (inferred) | 1661 | 871 = **52.4%** | +16.10 ms |
| `tapprobe` (tapped) | 1717 | 842 = **49.0%** | +15.84 ms |

And the two grids are the same grid: nearest-neighbour `tapprobe` beat → `lens6full` beat, **median |Δ| = 0.00 ms, 86.3% within 1 ms**. Per-slot means move ≤1.3 ms (slot 0: +7.66 → +8.07). The 0/274 → 275/277 headline is a **label flip at `beat.js:126-127`** (`taps.valid` ⇒ `drawing:"bars"` vs `inferred` needing `mode==="steady"`), not a different measurement. C1's "a supplied beat unlocks the entire take" does not support anything downstream.

**D2 — The C1 experiment fed the tracker its own output.** `scratchpad\taps.json` holds **1084 taps; `lens6full` has exactly 1084 beats**; tap → nearest inferred beat **median 0.000 ms, 91.8% exact, max 0.050 ms**. The proposal concedes "proving nothing about accuracy" but not that the supplied beat *was the inferred beat*. The Stage 1 pass bar ("≥90% metric bars, I measured 275/277") is calibrated on this and cannot fail.

**D3 — The swing statistic is circular, and it routes his actual swing out of the report.** "Swing measured on quantizer-confirmed `d=2` beats only" is selection on the dependent variable. Two-onset beats, first onset on the beat, n=289, tapped grid:

```
ALL 2-onset beats    n=289  median_x=0.515  sd_x=0.1126 beat (66.9 ms)
quantizer said d=2   n=182  median_x=0.507  sd_x=0.0364 beat (21.6 ms)   <- the design's 0.018-0.038
quantizer said d=3   n= 58  median_x=0.637  sd_x=0.1227 beat (72.9 ms)   <- UNCHANGED
```

Conditioning on d=2 collapses σ 3.1×; conditioning on d=3 does nothing. It is not reducing noise, it is picking the beats that already fit a 2-grid. So the report says "median x = 0.507, straight, not sloppy" while the 58 beats at **x = 0.637** (20% of pairs) are excluded *because* they swung. Unconditioned σ_x = 0.1126 is 1.9× the design's own `sloppy` threshold (0.06), so "sloppy" is the honest verdict and the pipeline is built so it can never be reached. This is the operator's stated fear — feel quantized away — happening inside the instrument built to measure feel. `claimable`/`ratio` cannot catch it; the conditioning is upstream of the ratio.

**D4 — Microtiming is not preserved at the beat. The grid eats anticipations.** Sign of deviation, tapped grid:

```
slot 0 (on the beat)  n=1717   EARLY  3.7%   ZERO 49.0%   LATE 47.3%   min -78, p1 -58.8
half beat             n=1205   EARLY 35.3%   ZERO  1.5%   LATE 63.2%   min -83
triplet 1/3, 2/3      n= 435   EARLY 42.5%   ZERO  0.5%   LATE 57.0%   min -82.7
notes early beyond -12.5% of a beat at slot 0:  0
```

Off-beat slots are near-symmetric; slot 0 is not. "On top of vs behind the beat" *at the beat* is the drummer's feel claim, and it is the one the grid destroys by construction — plus `nextBeat: 0.875` (`quantize.js:39`, doc line 14) truncates the early tail and flips its sign into the next beat. A deviation ribbon over this data draws a wall of late ticks at every downbeat and calls it his feel.

**D5 — Triplet-vs-straight has a 23% floor at one beat; every gate in the design is per-beat.** sd_x = 0.1126 beat against a 1/3-vs-1/2 separation of 0.1667 beat: **sd/sep = 0.68**, Bayes error **23.0%** with equal priors — no algorithm beats it on a single beat. Pooling same-figure beats: 2→14.8%, 3→10.0%, 4→6.9%, 6→3.5%, 8→1.8%. The design's `margin` gate and `max_abs_dev_ms` gate both decide *per beat*, i.e. below the noise floor; phrase-level pooling appears only as a footnote about one edge case. On the core ask the design **restates the problem**: "resolvable only at phrase level by whether the 1/3 position is occupied anywhere in the window" is the answer, and it is not the architecture.

Worse, the modes it wants to resolve are not there. Fractional-position histogram (2764 groups, tapped grid, ±1/48 windows): 1/4 → 157, **1/3 → 85, trough between them (0.271–0.312) → 91**; 3/4 → 170, **2/3 → 87, trough (0.688–0.729) → 96**. The troughs are *fuller than the triplet peaks*. Only 0 (839) and ~0.48–0.50 (480 across two bins) are modes.

**D6 — The tuplet gate is set below one standard deviation and rejects 15%, not 3.** Gate = 0.25·(P/d) = **49.5 ms** at P=594, d=3, against sd 66.9 ms. On the 145 beats the quantizer already read as d=3: worst-member |dev| median 18.7, p90 54.9, max 108.7 → **22/145 = 15.2% flagged spurious**. The acceptance test ("flags the three known spurious `d=3` beats while keeping all 14 clean triplets") names 3 positives and 14 negatives out of a population of 145 and is satisfiable while 19 unexamined beats are demoted.

**D7 — `sigmaMaxMs: 60` bounds the per-beat-σ patch out of relevance.** `quantize.js:38`: `sigma0Ms: 40, sigmaMinMs: 15, sigmaMaxMs: 60`. Real spread on the pair statistic is 66.9 ms; |dev| p99 = 85.7, max 169.1. The σ EMA cannot reach the truth, so threading per-beat σ into `cleanDivisions` cannot fix a model that is 2× too confident. The proposal never mentions the clamp. Separately, "per-beat σ spans 2.7–3.2× within a segment" is **unverifiable from any artifact** — σ is per-segment in the export (`index.js:614`); observed across segments it spans 24.19–40 ms = **1.65×**, and two segments sit at exactly 40 (never adapted).

**D8 — The seam claim fails for the fields the gates need.** The export carries `id, bar, pos, t_ms, voice, staff, dur` and `measures[].beats_ms` (verified) — so Stage 1's `dev_ms`/`dev_beat`/`slot`/`P` are derivable. But `sigma`, `margin`, `runnerUp` and per-beat `d` are **not in the export**; `measures[].tuplets` is only `{voice, beat, actual, normal}` and only for bracketed beats. "It consumes the export, not internal state… that is the seam" holds for Stage 1 and breaks for every gate in Stage 2, which needs an export-schema change the proposal never lists.

**D9 — The tap alignment offset is unmeasured, hand-entered, and larger than the signal.** `taps_from_session.py --offset-ms` has no source: nothing in `events.jsonl` ties a tap session's clock to playback position. `replay.js:8,13` — `position() = offset + (performance.now()-began)*speed`, with a `speed` control and a `seek` — so the map is piecewise-affine, and `offset`/`began`/`speed` are never logged. On top of that the design never names two systematic biases of exactly the size it reports (+2 to +20 ms): audio output latency, and sensorimotor **negative mean asynchrony** in tapping to an auditory sequence (−20 to −50 ms, textbook). And taps arrive through the same `onMidiMessage` (`piano.js:3513`) that discards `ev.timeStamp` — the handler jitter the design defers to a "prerequisite" contaminates the reference itself, not just the notes.

**D10 — Real taps will not select an existing agent.** `beat.js:415` requires `|a.p/p − 1| ≤ tapSelect (0.08)`; `beat.js:412` derives `p` from the median of **3** gaps, clamped to 40–200 bpm. Measured period spread on this take is p90/p10 = **1.66** (bpm 81→135). So real human taps will usually fall outside 8% of any live agent and seed a naive constant-period agent at `score = 1.1 × top` (`beat.js:421-424`) — a *worse* grid than the inferred one, not a reference. Nothing in the design tests this; the synthetic run couldn't, because its taps were the agents' own beats.

**D11 — The premise the design calls mandatory is not in the data.** The brief's "gaps cluster at 120 ms and 160 ms, with 240 and 320 behind" does not survive. On merged onset groups (40 ms, matching `performance.py:41`) the top IOIs are a flat continuum: 184×32, 160×32, 176×30, 162×29, 174×29, 164×28, 148×28, 182×27, 178×27, 156×27, 144×27, 150×27, 136×27 — no bimodality. Raw note gaps: exact 120 → **11**, exact 160 → **29**, 240 → 5, 320 → 14, out of 4524 nonzero; bands 110–130 → 150, 130–150 → 256, 150–170 → 309, 170–190 → 240. The 120/160 figure is chord spread (top raw gaps are 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 ms). "Two subdivisions of one pulse coexisting… any design MUST handle this case explicitly" was accepted without a receipt.

Steady-mode bars are **7.7%** (inferred) / 7.9% (tapped), not "0–6.8%". "264 tuplet groups came out" is offered as evidence the brackets work — the *inferred* run produced **285**, so taps removed 21 with no correctness check either way. `serve.py:345` is `/api/performance/open`; the events route is `:347-349`.

**Acceptance tests.** Stage 1: the bars criterion is circular (D2) and the beam criterion is a real, falsifiable one-liner. The tap-to-tap agreement test is the one genuinely good test in the document, and it is aimed at the right risk — but it is scheduled *after* the bars criterion has already passed, and with D9 unresolved a stable offset error would let two passes agree while both are wrong by the same constant. ## Verdict

**Modify, hard — one stage survives and the rest is built on a measurement that does not exist.** The engineering craft here is real and the corrections C2/C4/C6 are honest work, but the load-bearing axiom is falsified by the proposal's own artifact: taps change a label, not a grid (49.0% vs 52.4% exact-zero, 86.3% of beats identical to within 1 ms), because `beat.js:198` drags the beat onto the onsets no matter which rung drew. Everything the design promises the operator — a per-slot profile, a swing verdict, a deviation ribbon — is then measured against a grid defined by the notes it is measuring, and the two places the numbers look good (conditioned σ_x 0.036, slot-0 mean +16 ms) are artifacts of that circularity and of selection on the dependent variable. What should be built instead, in order: (1) fix `musicxml.js:369` and read `ev.timeStamp` — both one-liners, both real; (2) build the *offset-and-drift receipt* for tap capture before any tap analysis, because without it Stage 1's artifact carries an unbounded bias where the signal is +2 to +20 ms; (3) drop `phaseGain` to 0 (or expose an unsnapped reference grid alongside the snapped display grid) — without that there is no non-circular denominator and no amount of downstream contract discipline creates one; (4) replace every per-beat gate with phrase-level figure classification, since 23% single-beat error is an information floor and pooling four beats is the only thing that clears it; (5) report swing unconditioned or not at all, and when σ_x = 0.113 beat say "sloppy" out loud — he is a drummer, he will respect that far more than a straight-eighths verdict produced by hiding the beats that swung. The `feel.json` contract, `grid.trust`, and the tap-twice test are all worth keeping; the build order is wrong end first.
