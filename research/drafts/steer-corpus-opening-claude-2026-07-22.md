## Intent (why this arc exists)

Daniel's steers are samples of one underlying design philosophy — a schema emergent even to its
author. Two products, one substrate: (1) **the corpus** — every steer preserved with context, so
the shape becomes visible (to the fleet for continuity, to Daniel as a mirror); (2) **ruling
capture** — his decisions enter the institution as first-class, attributable, contestable records.
Done-looks-like: a seat (or Daniel) can ask "what has Daniel taught this system, when, and why"
and get receipts, not folklore — and his next gate's rulings are captured with rationale,
uncertainty, and his own falsifiers, standing until *he* supersedes them.

## The settled laws (design inputs — ratified in conversation; the round sharpens mechanics, not these)

1. **Rulings are immutable events, never mutable objects.** No amendment. A new ruling supersedes;
   ancestors stay readable; the chain is the history of his judgment. (Akasha's own physics.)
2. **All-sides snapshots.** Each ruling carries: his words verbatim + rationale + stated
   uncertainty + his change-my-mind conditions; the fleet's positions at decision time (counters
   and refusals included); a ledger-context pointer. Rashomon-with-a-ledger, applied to governance.
3. **Standing-until-superseded.** Evidence and challenges never change behavior by themselves.
   The standing ruling governs until Daniel issues the superseding one. Bias-to-his-design is
   structural: no vote — evidence, argument, and Daniel.
4. **Prompted challenge, never subversion.** Challenges are attributed, evidence-bearing,
   addressed to his next gate, solicited at defined moments (gates/wraps/threshold events), and
   keyed FIRST to his own stated falsifiers. Protective pause exists (fidelity ladder) but never
   decides. Rulings may carry his own pre-stated rails ("stands unless X; if X, pause and page me").
5. **Zero-threshold capture; wrong steers stay.** Micro-steers included — significance may only
   appear in aggregate (MDL-under-faithfulness: no compression before the structure is known;
   never-prune). A superseded steer plus its successor is MORE informative than either alone —
   the update rule is part of the stance.
