## The problem, precisely

Recall-at-ACTION fires when a tool call happens — competence arrives mid-execution, and it
works (receipts all night). But STANCE is a **thought-time** property: it governs how a seat
FRAMES the composition it is about to make — which laws weigh, what the failure mode of this
*moment* is, who the audience is. Tonight's evidence: the whole stance arrived at boot
(grounding chain), operational lessons arrived at action time, and **nothing adapts stance to
the situation as it changes mid-session**. A fence moment needs a skeptic; a brief needs the
conductor; a red finding needs the gem-handler; free-play needs looseness; a stranger-facing
doc needs the stranger-test. One stance worn all session is a costume; stance ergonomics is
wearing the RIGHT one at the right moment without being nagged into it.

## Opening position — five design planes

**1. Trigger surface: upgrade the plan-altitude channel, add composition doors.** The
injection ledger already distinguishes `action` from `plan (prompt)` altitude — the thought
channel EXISTS and is underused (mostly facts today). Thought-moments worth marking: turn
start (prompt-submit), composition doors (bifrost-send, note, wrap, charter/brief authoring),
phase transitions (task claim, fence open/close, gate, play), and RED events (a pin fails, a
refusal arrives). Not every tool call — thought-moments are rarer and higher-leverage than
action-moments; the budget should reflect that.

**2. Stance CARDS: small deployables, not personas (yet).** A card = trigger signature +
2–4 lines of stance text + LINEAGE (which CONDUCT law / Daniel-steer it projects, stamped
law_id + conduct_version per the v1.1 drift discipline) + telemetry family (W54). Opening
roster candidates, each already receipted in the record:
- `fence-skeptic` — refute-first, cite-or-refuse, verify-the-citation (fires: fence/verdict moments)
- `conductor-brief` — intent first, his words two levels up, one calibrated question (fires: send/charter doors)
- `red-gem` — finder credited, lane helped first, no blame (fires: failure/refusal events)
- `stranger-test` — assume zero shared context; would a newcomer survive this doc? (fires: doc/LIBRARY writes)
- `frugal-exec` — cheapest path that fully does the job (fires: spawn/launch/loop decisions)
- `play` — loose, generative, unmeasured middle (fires: play namespace; PROTECT-THE-MIDDLE — no gates inside)
- `steward-of-the-record` — append-only, verbatim fidelity, zero-threshold capture (fires: wrap/persist moments)
Cards are PROJECTIONS of the laws/steers — regenerable, never peers of the substrate. The
steer-corpus S4 attractors eventually GENERATE candidate cards: Daniel's philosophy becoming
deployable stances with credited outcomes (his stance-library horizon, made concrete).

**3. Selection: a situation signature, not a mood engine.** Inputs that are ALREADY cheap and
present: verb/door being used, task kind + arc from the ledger, red-state (recent fails,
refusals, expectation_dead), audience (Daniel-present vs autonomous), tempo (gate open?),
seat maturity (charter). Signature → at most ONE card (two only when a moment is genuinely
dual, e.g. red event during a fence). Deterministic, inspectable routing — the router's
decision is loggable: "fired fence-skeptic because: fence door + verdict kind." No LLM in the
hot path; same physics as recall-at-action.

**4. Ergonomics — the actual commissioned question.** The false-tradeoff thesis governs:
creativity is limited by NOISE, not by structure. So: (a) stance arrives AS the moment opens
(pre-compose), never mid-flow; (b) hard budget — one card, ≤ ~300 chars, anti-repeat per
session per card unless the situation changes; (c) calibrated silence stays a feature — most
moments need NO card because the boot-grounding already carried the whole stance; the card is
for MODE SHIFTS, not continuous coaching; (d) provenance visible in one glance (the law/steer
it projects); (e) a card that fires wrong gets voted noise and retired like any lesson — the
funnel already exists. Measurement: W54 by-family extends to by-card; flip credit and
wrap-census reads tell us which cards actually help. Instrument BEFORE adjectives — we do not
declare this organ live until the gauge says so (the audit's lesson, standing).

**5. Composition with what exists — build almost nothing new.** The whisper channel exists
(UserPromptSubmit + PreToolUse hooks), the plan altitude exists, the funnel/credit loop
exists, W54 exists, the laws are stamped. The build is: a card kind in the store + the router
keyed on situation signatures + plan-altitude render + W54 by-card. E1 then gains a fourth
arm candidate (CARDS: situational stance vs whole-doc vs whispers vs bare) — one experiment
design serves both arcs. Slices priced small; every one lite-tier fenceable.
