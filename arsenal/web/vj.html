Mapping

Sets

    Click a set to switch to it. On the KeyLab, rewind and fast-forward switch to the previous and next scene (on the press; our FL script keeps them from FL; ?transport=off hands them back to the bindings below); R is next too. The main dial picks the laser gate now (its click is AUTO); a scene dial can be learned below. A switch lands on the next bar or beat, in the style chosen under Transitions and look. Fader 8 mixes toward the cued set by hand; after a switch it picks up again once you pull it to the bottom.

    Set parameters

    The KeyLab's knobs nudge these (relative), learn any other control below.

    Pads

    Velocity shapes each effect, through a steep curve: a soft tap is a nudge, a hard hit punches the whole frame, shakes it and dips the scene for a moment under the accent's light (the dip is held under the flash line by measuring the scene). Pads v2: velocity is size, a second press within a bar escalates (a third bigger still), every one-shot ends on the beat grid and repeats never land in the same place. Row 1 hits: 1 Ring (held, it ripples on every beat), 2 Lasers v2 (soft single, medium one-two, hard triple; faster presses roll on the grid; held, the section's loop), 3 CO2 (jets from the floor, held up to 3 s), 4 Glitch Lift (the frame jumps up; held, it strikes on every beat). Row 2 holds (held half a beat: a standing motion; a tap plays a short version): 5 Shutters, 6 Fog + Swarm, 7 Morph, 8 Lower. Row 3: 9 Safety (tap dampen, hard tap choke, hold black), 10 Hero (once per 8 bars, on the next ONE), 11 Build / Drop (a tap builds 4, 8 or 16 bars by velocity; held, a riser that drops on release), 12 the Zoom punch, unless the engine says pad 12 taps the tempo (the default: then it taps, and fires no effect). The white flash, Invert, Dampen and Choke stay in the list below. Kill stays on E, Dampen and Choke on Q and W; the colour pair on [ (Colour Next) and ] (Colour Same). Both pad sources work: the DAW-mode pads (DAW port, channel 10) and the normal pad bank (MIDI port, channel 10, notes 36–47). While FL Studio's Arturia script runs, the DAW-mode pads 1–8 also toggle FL step-sequencer steps when released: switch the KeyLab to its pad bank to keep FL's sequencer untouched.

    Faders and knobs

    Learn on touch: click Learn, then move a fader or knob. Any control the engine reports works.

    Options

    Flash safety is always on: every frame passes the flash limiter last (fewer than three flashes a second over any quarter of the screen, wherever it sits, red included, and a short rest after about five seconds of flashing). It only ever takes light away, and only when the picture would flash too often. Until the limiter runs (a moment after the page opens), and for good if this GPU cannot run it, pad effects are refused.
