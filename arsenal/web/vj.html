Mapping

Sets

    Click a set to switch to it. R or pad 12 switches to the next one, on the next bar or beat, in the style chosen under Transitions and look. Fader 8 mixes toward the cued set by hand; after a switch it picks up again once you pull it to the bottom.

    Set parameters

    The KeyLab's knobs nudge these (relative), learn any other control below.

    Pads

    Velocity shapes each effect; holding a pad sustains the holdable ones until you let go. Both pad sources work: the DAW-mode pads (DAW port, channel 10) and the normal pad bank (MIDI port, channel 10, notes 36–47). While FL Studio's Arturia script runs, the DAW-mode pads 1–8 also toggle FL step-sequencer steps when released: switch the KeyLab to its pad bank to keep FL's sequencer untouched.

    Faders and knobs

    Learn on touch: click Learn, then move a fader or knob. Any control the engine reports works.
