Why the beats matter. The score engine at score/index.js:102 accepts a fixed beats array and bypasses its own tracker entirely. That tracker snaps each beat 80% onto your nearest note (beat.js:74), so measuring your timing against it measures the snapping. These beats come from the click instead, so they are independent of what you played.

Times are the audible click — scheduled time plus this device’s output latency, shown above. Tapping ahead of a click is not corrected: humans land 20–50 ms early on purpose, and that is your relationship to the beat, not an error to hide.
