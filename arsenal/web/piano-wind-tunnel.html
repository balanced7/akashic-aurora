About the model & simulation

Car Concept by Eric Chadwick / Darmstadt Graphics Group, from Khronos’s glTF collection. Model & credits ↗ · CC BY 4.0.

A 3D incompressible flow solver transports velocity and solves pressure around a sealed exterior approximation of the car. Fine surface distances guide the light close to the mesh. Optional wake stirring is an artistic force.

These are evolving instantaneous streamlines. Light travel and tail length control their illumination; the worker advances simulation at its own pace. Air speed colours run from indigo (slow) through violet to warm pearl (fast). This prototype does not predict drag, lift, or resolve turbulent boundary layers.
