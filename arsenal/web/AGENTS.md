# Arsenal visual work

Before designing or changing the visuals under this directory, read
[the visual quality baseline](../VISUAL-QUALITY-BASELINE.md) and the relevant component design notes.
Daniel requested that the fidelity, quality, beauty and elegance achieved in the
2026-09-21 piano/Gyre work become the baseline for future work.

Carry forward the method: clear focal forms, layered motion, meaningful controls,
recoverable presets, native-resolution detail and measured frame delivery.
Use the recorded recipes as comparison references while allowing new visual identities.

For changed visuals, inspect the actual render in motion and report the dimensions,
timing and limits of the checks that apply. A passing suite alone does not establish
visual quality. Preserve the user's live view and saved work during verification.
The baseline's historical measurements are reference evidence, never a fresh pass.
